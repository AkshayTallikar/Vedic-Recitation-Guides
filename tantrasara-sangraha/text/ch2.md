The nyāsa with the fifty letters is (effective) "for any mantra whatsoever" — for every mantra, that is. "Vīryada": it bestows vīrya — power, whose mark is the deity's grace — hence "power-giving." "Ke" — on the head. "Abhito vaktram" — all around the face. (1–2)

When japa is to be performed, at the beginning one should carry out the drying and the rest with the bījas of Vāyu and the others. "In their maṇḍala" — in the maṇḍalas of Vāyu and the rest: having, with the bījas of Vāyu and Agni, performed the drying-up and the burning of the man of sin dwelling in the body, one should, with the bīja of Varuṇa, contemplate one's own body being flooded with nectar. #V
तदात्मनि हरिं ध्यात्वा कुण्डे देवीं श्रियं तथा ।
विष्णुवीर्यात्मकं वह्निं निक्षिपेत्प्रणवेन ह ॥ १९ ॥
#V-END
#T
"Having meditated on Hari as its very self, and likewise on the goddess Śrī in the kuṇḍa, one should cast in, with the praṇava, the fire that is of the essence of Viṣṇu's vigor. (19)
#T-END

#C
वह्निस्थापनमाह । तदात्मनीति । तदात्मनि । तद्रूप उक्तलक्षणे कुण्डले हरिं देवीं श्रियं च ध्यात्वा प्रणवेन विष्णुवीर्यात्मकवह्निं निक्षिपेत् ॥ १९ ॥
#C-END
#CT
He states the installation of the fire: "tadātmani." "In its self" — having meditated upon Hari, and upon the goddess Śrī, in the kuṇḍa of the aforesaid character which is His form, one should cast in, with the praṇava, the fire which is of the essence of Viṣṇu's vigor. (19)
#CT-END

#V
कुर्यात्क्रियाः षोडश च व्याहृतिभिः पृथक् पृथक् ।
वह्नेस्तद्धरेः प्रीतिं कुर्वन् द्रव्यैर्यजेत्ततः ॥ २० ॥
#V-END
#T
"And one should perform the sixteen rites for the fire, each separately, with the vyāhṛtis; then, effecting thereby the pleasure of Hari whose form it is, one should sacrifice with the offering-substances. (20)
#T-END

#C
कुर्यादिति । व्याहृतिभिः व्यस्तसमस्ताभिः वह्नेः षोडशसंस्कारान् गर्भाधानादीन् कुर्यात् । गर्भाधानं पुंसवनं सीमन्तो वैष्णवो बलिः । जातकं नामनिष्क्रामोऽन्नप्राशनं चौलकर्म च । उपनीतिर्महानाम्नी महाव्रतमतः परम् । उपाकर्म च गोदानं समावर्तमतः परम् । विवाहश्चेति विष्णुतीर्थीये । अन्यत्रापि विवाहान्ताः शुभे प्रोक्ता अशुभे मरणान्त्यका इति । तत्पक्षे विष्णुबलिर्नास्तीति द्रष्टव्यम् । एकैकसंस्कारार्थं चतस्रश्चतस्रः आहुतीर्दद्यात् ॥ २० ॥
#C-END
#CT
"Kuryāt": with the vyāhṛtis, severally and jointly, one should perform for the fire the sixteen saṁskāras, garbhādhāna and the rest. (As enumerated) in the Viṣṇutīrthīya: "Garbhādhāna, puṁsavana, sīmanta, the Vaiṣṇava bali; jātaka, the naming, the first going-out, the first feeding of rice, and the tonsure; the initiation (upanayana), the Mahānāmnī, and thereafter the Mahāvrata; the upākarma, the godāna, and thereafter the samāvartana; and marriage." Elsewhere too it is said that (the saṁskāras) ending with marriage are prescribed for the auspicious (rite), while in the inauspicious they end with the death-rites; on that view, be it noted, there is no Viṣṇu-bali. For each single saṁskāra one should offer four oblations apiece. (20)
#CT-END

#V
आज्येन वा पायसेन समिद्भिः क्षीरिणामथ ।
तिलैर्वा तण्डुलैर्वाऽपि मधुरैस्त्रिभिरेव वा ॥ २१ ॥
#V-END
#T
"With clarified butter, or with pāyasa, or with fuel-sticks of the milky trees, or with sesame, or even with rice-grains, or with just the three sweets. (21)
#T-END

#C
होमद्रव्याण्याह । आज्येनेति । आज्येन गव्येन पायसेन वा गोक्षीरसिद्धेन । क्षीरिणां औदुम्बरादीनां समिद्भिः । त्रिभिः मधुरैः घृतमधुशर्करादिभिः ॥ २१ ॥
#C-END
#CT
He states the substances for the homa: "ājyena." With ājya from the cow; or with pāyasa cooked in cow's milk; with fuel-sticks of the milky trees, the udumbara and the rest; "with the three sweets" — ghee, honey, sugar, and the like. (21)
#CT-END

#V
ब्रह्मवृक्षसमिद्भिर्वा समिद्भिर्वाऽमृतोद्भवैः ।
अमृताः समिधो ज्ञेयाः सर्वत्र चतुरङ्गुलाः ॥ २२ ॥
#V-END
#T
"Or with fuel-sticks of the brahma-tree, or with sticks sprung from the amṛtā; the amṛtā-sticks are to be known, in every case, as four aṅgulas long. (22)
#T-END

#C
ब्रह्मवृक्षः पलाशः । अमृताः गुडूचीसमिधः । चतुरङ्गुलमिताः सर्वहोमेषु ॥ २२ ॥
#C-END
#CT
The brahma-tree is the palāśa. "Amṛtāḥ" — fuel-sticks of the guḍūcī; measuring four aṅgulas in all homas. (22)
#CT-END

#V
एवमेव तु दीक्षायामाज्येनैवाहुतिक्रिया ।
पूजा च कार्या विधिवत्पूर्वं सर्वहुतेष्वपि ॥ २३ ॥
गुरवे दक्षिणां दद्याल्लक्षे लक्षे शतं सुधीः ।
आत्मनैव कृते होमे शक्तितो गुरुदक्षिणाम् ॥ २४ ॥
#V-END
#T
"In just the same way, at initiation (dīkṣā) the offering of oblations is with ājya alone; and in all fire-offerings too, worship must first be duly performed according to rule. (23) The wise man should give the teacher a fee of one hundred for every hundred thousand (oblations); when the homa has been performed by oneself, (one should still give) the guru's fee according to one's means. (24)
#T-END

#H इति श्रीमदानन्दतीर्थभगवत्पादाचार्यविरचिते श्रीमत्तन्त्रसारसङ्ग्रहे द्वितीयोऽध्यायः ॥ २ ॥
#T
Thus the second chapter of the revered Tantrasārasaṅgraha composed by the revered Ānandatīrtha Bhagavatpādācārya. (2)
#T-END

#H इति श्रीमध्वमाधवानुग्रहैकसहायवेदवेद्यशिष्यविरचिते श्रीमत्तन्त्रसारसङ्ग्रहव्याख्याने तन्त्रसारविवृतौ द्वितीयोऽध्यायः समाप्तः ॥ २ ॥
#T
Thus, in the Tantrasāravivṛti — the exposition of the revered Tantrasārasaṅgraha composed by the disciple of Vedavedya, whose sole support is the grace of Madhva and Mādhava — the second chapter is complete. (2)
