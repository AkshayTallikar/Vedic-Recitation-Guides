#P 20
#H ॥ अथ तृतीयोऽध्यायः ॥
#T
Now the Third Chapter.
#T-END

#V
प्रतिष्ठां कारयन् विष्णोः कुर्यात् सुप्रतिमां बुधः ।
लोहैर्वा शिलया दार्वा मृदा वापि यथाक्रमम् ॥ १ ॥
#V-END
#T
"The wise man, when having an installation (pratiṣṭhā) of Viṣṇu performed, should have a fine image made — of metals, or of stone, or of wood, or even of clay, in that order of preference. (1)
#T-END

#C
प्रतिमाप्रतिष्ठां विवक्षुः अस्मिन्नध्याये प्रतिमालक्षणमाह । प्रतिष्ठामिति । प्रतिष्ठां कारयन् । कारयिष्यन् । लोहैः - सुवर्णादिधातुभिः । दार्वा । चन्दनादिकाष्ठेन ॥ १ ॥
#C-END
#CT
Wishing to expound the installation of images, he states in this chapter the characteristics of the image: "pratiṣṭhām." "Causing the installation" — being about to have it performed. "With metals" — with gold and the other metals. "Of wood" — with sandal or other such timber. (1)
#CT-END

#V
षण्णवत्यङ्गुलां योग आसीनामथवा स्थिताम् ।
शयानां वा मुमुक्षूणां व्याचक्षाणां निजां स्थितिम् ॥ २ ॥
#V-END
#T
"Ninety-six aṅgulas tall; seated in yoga, or else standing, or reclining — proclaiming to the seekers of liberation their own (true) state. (2)
#T-END

#C
षण्णवत्यङ्गुलपरिमितां । योगे योगपदे आसीनां । मुमुक्षूणां निजां स्थितिं । स्वस्वरूपं व्याचक्षाणां शयानां वा व्याचक्षाणामित्यन्वयः ॥ २ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"Measuring ninety-six aṅgulas." "In yoga" — seated in the yoga posture. "Proclaiming to the seekers of liberation their own state" — declaring (to each) his own essential nature; or the construction is: "reclining, and proclaiming." (2)
#CT-END

#V
मुखादूर्ध्वं व्यङ्गुलोच्चां नवाङ्गुललसन्मुखाम् ।
सुवृत्तत्र्यङ्गुलग्रीवामास्तनाच्च षडङ्गुलाम् ॥ ३ ॥
#V-END
#T
"Above the face, two aṅgulas high (the crown); her shining face of nine aṅgulas; her neck well-rounded, of three aṅgulas; and six aṅgulas down to the breast; (3)
#T-END

#C
षण्णवत्यङ्गुलत्वमाह । मुखादूर्ध्वमित्यादि सहार्धचतुरङ्गुलामित्यन्तेन । मुखादूर्ध्वं शिरःपर्यन्तं व्यङ्गुलोच्चां नवाङ्गुलपरिमितं लसन् मुखं यस्याः सा तां । सुवृत्ता त्र्यङ्गुलपरिमिता ग्रीवा यस्याः सा तां । आस्तनाच्च स्तनपर्यन्तं ग्रीवाया अधस्तात् षडङ्गुलां ॥ ३ ॥
#C-END
#CT
He sets forth the make-up of the ninety-six aṅgulas, from "mukhād ūrdhvam" down to "sahārdha-caturaṅgulām." Above the face — up to the top of the head — two aṅgulas high; that (image) whose gleaming face measures nine aṅgulas; whose neck is well-rounded, measuring three aṅgulas; "and down to the breast" — six aṅgulas from below the neck to the breast. (3)
#CT-END

#V
पञ्चदशाङ्गुलां नाभेरासार्द्धदशकाङ्गुलाम् ।
वृषणादामूलतश्च सार्धव्यङ्गुलमायताम् ॥ ४ ॥
#V-END
#T
"fifteen aṅgulas to the navel; from there to the scrotum ten and a half aṅgulas; and from (the scrotum) to the root, two and a half aṅgulas in extent; (4)
#T-END

#C
नाभेः आनाभिपर्यन्तं स्तनादधस्तात् पञ्चदशाङ्गुलां । वृषणात् आ वृषणपर्यन्तं नाभेरधस्तात् सार्धदशाङ्गुलां । आमूलतः मूलाधारपर्यन्तं सार्धव्यङ्गुलमायताम् । आ आ मूलतः इति पदच्छेदः । एकस्यैवाङो देहलीदीपन्यायेन उभयसम्बन्धो वा ॥ ४ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"From the navel" — that is, up to the navel, below the breast: fifteen aṅgulas. "From the scrotum" — up to the scrotum, below the navel: ten and a half aṅgulas. "Āmūlataḥ" — as far as the mūlādhāra: two and a half aṅgulas in extent. The word-division is "ā mūlataḥ" (with the prefix ā); or else the one prefix āṅ connects with both terms, by the maxim of the lamp set on the threshold (serving both rooms). (4)
#CT-END

#V
तावदा गुदतो दीर्घां चैत्यादष्टादशाङ्गुलाम् ।
तथा व्यङ्गुलचैत्यां च द्वाविंशजङ्घया युताम् ॥ ५ ॥
#V-END
#T
"as much again in length down to the anus; eighteen aṅgulas from the caitya (knee); likewise with a knee-region of two aṅgulas, and furnished with a shank of twenty-two; (5)
#T-END

#C
आगुदतः अपानपर्यन्तं तावत् सार्धव्यङ्गुलदीर्घां । चैत्यात् जानुतः आजानुपर्यन्तं अष्टादशाङ्गुलां । तथा व्यङ्गुलचैत्यां व्यङ्गुलपरिमितजानुदेशां । द्वाविंशदङ्गुलमितजङ्घया युताम् ॥ ५ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"From the anus" — up to the apāna: that much, two and a half aṅgulas long. "From the caitya" — from the knee: (the thigh) reaching to the knee, eighteen aṅgulas. "Likewise with a two-aṅgula caitya" — having a knee-region measuring two aṅgulas; joined with a shank measuring twenty-two aṅgulas. (5)
#CT-END

#P 21
#V
आरभ्य गुल्फमध्यं च सहार्धचतुरङ्गुलाम् ।
षडङ्गुलोच्चप्रपदां सार्धव्यङ्गुलमेव च ॥ ६ ॥
#V-END
#T
"and, beginning from the middle of the ankle, four and a half aṅgulas; the fore-foot six aṅgulas high — and (its middle) just two and a half. (6)
#T-END

#C
गुल्फमारभ्य सार्धचतुरङ्गुलां । प्राचीनैस्तु सार्धचतुरङ्गुलं प्रपदं स्यादिति व्याख्यातं । षडङ्गुलोच्चप्रपदादिभागाम् । सार्धत्र्यङ्गुलप्रपदमध्याम् ॥ ६ ॥
#C-END
#CT
Beginning from the ankle, four and a half aṅgulas. The ancients, however, explained that the fore-foot (prapada) should be four and a half aṅgulas. She whose initial portion of the fore-foot is six aṅgulas high; whose middle of the fore-foot is three and a half aṅgulas. (6)
#CT-END

#V
प्रपदोच्चयुतामन्ते व्यङ्गुलप्रपदोच्छ्रयाम् ।
त्र्यङ्गुलव्यङ्गुलामर्धहीनमध्याङ्गुलामपि ॥ ७ ॥
#V-END
#T
"joined with the fore-foot's height — at its end an elevation of two aṅgulas; her pair of toes (the great toe and the next) of three aṅgulas; and the middle toe less by a half. (7)
#T-END

#C
अन्ते प्रपदाग्रे व्यङ्गुलप्रपदोच्छ्रयां । त्र्यङ्गुलव्यङ्गुलां त्र्यङ्गुलमिते द्वे अङ्गुल्यौ अङ्गुष्ठं तदनन्तराङ्गुलिश्च यस्यास्ताम् । अर्धहीनमध्याङ्गुलाम् । अर्धहीनं अङ्गुलं सार्धव्यङ्गुलं तत्परिमिता मध्याङ्गुलिर्यस्याः ताम् ॥ ७ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"At the end" — at the tip of the fore-foot, an elevation of two aṅgulas. "Tryaṅgula-vyaṅgulām": she whose two digits — the great toe and the toe immediately following it — measure three aṅgulas each. "With the middle toe less by a half": the measure less a half, that is, two and a half aṅgulas — her middle toe is of that measure. (7)
#CT-END

#V
त्रिपादाङ्गुलहीनान्यां ततस्तादृशतत्पराम् ।
पादोनोच्चाङ्गुष्ठनखां तदर्धतदनन्तराम् ॥ ८ ॥
#V-END
#T
"the next (toe) less by three quarters of an aṅgula; then, following it, one of like measure (less a quarter); the nail of her great toe three quarters (of an aṅgula) high, and the one next to it half of that. (8)
#T-END

#C
त्रिपादाङ्गुलिहीनान्यां । त्रिभिः पादैः हीनं अङ्गुलं सपादाङ्गुलिद्वयमित्यर्थः । तत्परिमिता अन्या अनामिका यस्यास्ताम् । ततः तादृशतत्परां ततो अनामिकातः तादृशी पादन्यूना । तत्परा कनिष्ठाङ्गुलिर्यस्यास्ताम् । अङ्गुलिद्वयदीर्घकनिष्ठाङ्गुलमित्यर्थः । पादोनोच्चाङ्गुष्ठनखां । पादोनैकाङ्गुलोन्नताङ्गुष्ठनखाम् । तदर्धतदनन्तराम् । सार्धैकपादोच्चतदनन्तराम् । अयं भावः । अङ्गुष्ठनखस्य त्रिपादोच्चत्वम् । तदनन्तराङ्गुलिनखस्य सार्धैकपादोच्चत्वं । तदनन्तरस्य सार्धैकपादस्य चतुर्धा विभागे कृते त्रिभागोच्चत्वम् । तदनन्तरयोरपि तद्वदिति ॥ ८ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"Less by three quarters of an aṅgula": an aṅgula diminished by three quarters — the sense being two digits and a quarter (2¼ aṅgulas); she whose "other" toe, the anāmikā (fourth), is of that measure. "Then, following it, one of like kind": after the anāmikā, one like it but less by a quarter — she whose next toe, the little toe, is such; that is, the little toe is two aṅgulas long. "With the great-toe's nail a quarter less (an aṅgula) high": her great toe's nail is raised one aṅgula less a quarter. "The next, half of that": the one following is one and a half quarters high. The purport is this: the great toe's nail has a height of three quarters; the nail of the toe next to it, a height of one and a half quarters; for the one after that, when that one-and-a-half-quarter measure is divided into four parts, its height is three of those parts; and for the two after that, likewise. (8)
#CT-END

#V
क्रमशः पादहीनान्यां रक्तपादनखां शुभाम् ।
विस्तारः प्रपदस्यापि षडङ्गुल उदाहृतः ॥ ९ ॥
#V-END
#T
"the others, in order, each less by a quarter; her toe-nails red; auspicious. And the breadth of the fore-foot is declared to be six aṅgulas. (9)
#T-END

#C
तदेव विशदयति । क्रमश इति । क्रमेण पादहीनान्यां । रक्ताः शोणाः पादनखाः यस्यास्ताम् । अनुक्तानामङ्गानां परिमाणमाह । विस्तार इति । प्रपदस्य पादाग्रस्य षडङ्गुलपरिमितो विस्तारः ॥ ९ ॥
#C-END
#CT
He makes that very point clear: "kramaśaḥ" — in order, each diminished by a quarter. She whose toe-nails are red, crimson. He then states the measure of the limbs not yet mentioned: "vistāraḥ." Of the prapada — the fore-part of the foot — the breadth measures six aṅgulas. (9)
#CT-END

#V
अङ्गुष्ठपरिणाहस्तु चतुरङ्गुल ईरितः ।
त्र्यङ्गुलस्तु तदन्यस्या व्रीह्यर्धोनाः क्रमात् पराः ॥ १० ॥
#V-END
#T
"The girth of the great toe is declared to be four aṅgulas; of the next one, three aṅgulas; and the rest, in order, are less by half a barley-corn each. (10)
#T-END

#C
अङ्गुष्ठपरिणाहस्तु चतुरङ्गुलः । तदन्यस्याः अङ्गुष्ठादन्यस्याः त्र्यङ्गुलपरिणाहः परिमितो विस्तारः । पराः अङ्गुलयः क्रमात् व्रीह्यर्धोनाः व्रीह्यर्धहीनत्र्यङ्गुलपरिणाहाः ॥ १० ॥
#C-END
#CT
The girth of the great toe is four aṅgulas. Of the one other than it — other than the great toe — the girth, its measured breadth, is three aṅgulas. The remaining toes, in order, are "less by half a vrīhi" — each having a girth of three aṅgulas diminished (successively) by half a barley-grain. (10)
#CT-END

#V
दशाङ्गुलं जङ्घमूलं तन्मध्ये त्र्यङ्गुलाधिकम् ।
सप्तदशाङ्गुलं तूर्ध्वं जानुरष्टादशाङ्गुलः ॥ ११ ॥
#V-END
#T
"The base of the shank ten aṅgulas (in girth); at its middle three aṅgulas more; and above, seventeen aṅgulas; the knee eighteen aṅgulas. (11)
#T-END

#C
दशाङ्गुलं जङ्घमूलं । जङ्घयोर्मूलं दशाङ्गुलपरिणाहम् । जङ्घयोर्मध्यं त्र्यङ्गुलाधिकदशाङ्गुलपरिणाहं । ऊर्ध्वं जङ्घावसानं सप्तदशाङ्गुलपरिणाहम् । जानुप्रदेशस्तु अष्टादशाङ्गुलः ॥ ११ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"The base of the shank, ten aṅgulas": the base of the two shanks has a girth of ten aṅgulas. The middle of the shanks has a girth of ten aṅgulas plus three. Above — the top end of the shank — has a girth of seventeen aṅgulas. The knee-region is eighteen aṅgulas. (11)
#CT-END

#V
ऊर्वन्तोऽपि तथा ज्ञेयो मध्यं द्विद्वादशाङ्गुलम् ।
चतुरङ्गुलाधिकं मूलं गुह्यं सार्धचतुष्टयम् ॥ १२ ॥
#V-END
#T
"The end of the thigh too is to be known as the same (eighteen); its middle twenty-four aṅgulas; its base four aṅgulas more (twenty-eight); the private part four and a half in length. (12)
#T-END

#C
ऊर्वन्तः जानूपरिभागोऽपि तथा अष्टाङ्गुलपरिणाहः । ऊरुमध्यं द्विद्वादशाङ्गुलं चतुर्विंशत्यङ्गुलपरिणाहं । ऊरुमूलं चतुरङ्गुलाधिकं अष्टाविंशत्यङ्गुलपरिणाहम् । गुह्यं लिङ्गं सार्धचतुष्टयं दीर्घम् ॥ १२ ॥
#C-END
#CT
"The end of the thigh" — the part above the knee too has (the same, i.e.) a girth of eighteen aṅgulas. The middle of the thigh is "twice twelve," a girth of twenty-four aṅgulas. The base of the thigh is four aṅgulas more, a girth of twenty-eight aṅgulas. The private part — the phallus — is four and a half (aṅgulas) long. (12)
#CT-END

#V
सार्धत्रयं परिणाहाद्वृषणं चतुरङ्गुलम् ।
सप्ताङ्गुलं परिणाहाद्वृत्तं प्रोक्तं समस्तशः ॥ १३ ॥
#V-END
#T
"three and a half in girth; the scrotum four aṅgulas (long), seven aṅgulas in girth, declared to be rounded on all sides. (13)
#T-END

#C
परिणाहस्तु सार्धत्र्यङ्गुलः । वृषणं सार्धचतुरङ्गुलदीर्घम् । परिणाहस्तु सप्ताङ्गुलम् ॥ १३ ॥
#C-END
#CT
Its girth is three and a half aṅgulas. The scrotum is four and a half aṅgulas long; its girth is seven aṅgulas. (13)
#CT-END

#V
अष्टत्रिंशाङ्गुलः कट्याः परिणाह उदाहृतः ।
सुवृत्तत्वं तथा श्रोण्योः पीनत्वं च समत्वतः ॥ १४ ॥
#V-END
#T
"The girth of the waist is declared to be thirty-eight aṅgulas; likewise the well-roundedness of the two buttocks and their plumpness, evenly. (14)
#T-END

#C
कट्याः परिणाहस्तु अष्टत्रिंशाङ्गुल । श्रोण्याः पीनत्वं सुवृत्तत्वं च । समत्वतः समत्वेन ॥ १४ ॥
#C-END
#CT
The girth of the waist is thirty-eight aṅgulas. Of the buttocks, plumpness and well-roundedness; "samatvataḥ" — with evenness. (14)
#CT-END

#V
विस्तारश्च तथा कट्याः सुप्रतिष्ठितता पदोः ।
कट्याश्चैवाङ्गुलाधिक्यं नाभेरध उदाहृतम् ॥ १५ ॥
#V-END
#T
"and likewise the breadth of the waist, and the firm well-set condition of the feet; and it is declared that the waist has one aṅgula more below the navel. (15)
#T-END

#C
कट्याः विस्तारः पदोः सुप्रतिष्ठत्वं च विधेयमिति शेषः । नाभेरधः कट्याः कटिपरिणाहात् एकाङ्गुलाधिक्यं परिणाहस्येति शेषः ॥ १५ ॥
#C-END
#CT
The breadth of the waist, and the firm well-set condition of the feet, are to be provided — supply "are to be made." "Below the navel" — the waist has, over the waist's girth, an excess of one aṅgula — supply "of girth." (15)
#CT-END

#V
नाभिः सार्धाङ्गुलश्चैव गम्भीरोर्धाङ्गुलोऽन्ततः ।

#V-END

#V-END
#T

#C-END
#CT
The girth of the arm is eighteen aṅgulas. That girth-measure decreases progressively downward. At its end — the end of the arm, the wrist-region — the girth is eight and a half aṅgulas. (20)
#CT-END

#V
साङ्गुलस्तु तलः सार्धनवाङ्गुल उदाहृतः ।
समं तदुभयं ज्ञेयं तथैव तलविस्तृतिः ॥ २१ ॥
#V-END
#T

#T-END

#C

#C-END
#CT
"Together with the fingers" — the palm with the fingers, the palm-surface, is nine and a half aṅgulas. Both of these — the fingers and the palm — are to be known as equal. The breadth of the palm too is to be known as the same. The purport is that the three — the breadth of the palm, the length of the palm, and the length of the fingers — are each equal, being four aṅgulas and three quarters. (21)
#CT-END

#V

#V-END
#T
"The middle (finger) is half an aṅgula high; of the two (adjoining) the height is one and a half aṅgulas; of that little finger, however, the next is more by a barley-corn. (22)

#C
मध्या मध्याङ्गुलिः सकाशात् अर्धाङ्गुलोच्चता । तस्याः कनिष्ठिकायाः सकाशात्परः अङ्गुष्ठः व्रीहिमात्राधिकः । व्रीहिमात्रादधिकः ॥ २२ ॥
#C-END
#CT
The middle finger, compared (with the palm), is half an aṅgula in height. Compared with that little finger, the next — the thumb — is more by a barley-corn: more by the measure of a barley-corn. (22)
#CT-END

#V
सार्धत्रयपरिणाहौ मध्यज्येष्ठावुदाहृतौ ।
अन्यौ व्रीहितदर्धोनावन्या सार्धद्वयाङ्गुला ॥ २३ ॥
#V-END
#T
"The middle and the greatest (thumb) are declared to have a girth of three and a half; the other two are less by a barley-corn and half of that; and the other (the little finger) is two and a half aṅgulas. (23)

#C

#C-END
#CT
The middle and the greatest — the middle finger and the thumb — have a girth of three and a half aṅgulas. The other two — those before and after the middle finger — have a girth less by one and a half barley-corns. Compared with the middle finger, the other, the little finger, has a girth of two and a half aṅgulas. (23)

#C-END
#CT
The palm-surface, the nails, the corner of the eye, the lower lip, the upper lip and the tongue are "rakta" — reddish. The two nails — the great-toe/thumb nails — are one aṅgula less a quarter in length. (26)
#CT-END

#V

#V-END
#T
"the two middle ones (nails) half an aṅgula; the others diminishing in order by an eighth-part. And below the nose, the nose and the rarāṭikā (philtrum) (27) are equal in measure; and in the measuring, by the height of the nose, everything of the face will come to half an aṅgula more. (28)
#T-END

#C
मध्यौ तर्जनीमध्यमयोर्नखौ अर्धाङ्गुलदीर्घौ । अन्ये अन्याङ्गुलिनखास्तु अष्टांशक्रमोनकाः । अष्टमांशेन क्रमतो न्यूनाः मध्यमनखापेक्षया अनामिकनखोऽष्टमांशेन न्यूनः । ततः कनिष्ठिकानख इति । नासिकाया अधस्तात् आचुबुकान्तं नासिका च रराटिका ललाटं च एते समानमानाः चुबुकमारभ्य नासाग्रे ललाटान्तपर्यन्तं मापने क्रियमाणे नासायां उच्चतया सर्वमुखस्य अर्धाङ्गुलोच्चत्वमित्यर्थः ॥ २७-२८ ॥

Below the nose down to the chin — the nose and the rarāṭikā (the furrow of the upper lip) and the forehead — these are of equal measure. When the measuring is done from the chin to the tip of the nose and up to the top of the forehead, by the height at the nose, the whole face comes to half an aṅgula in height — such is the meaning. #CT
Of these tattva-mantras a threefold repetition is to be made in the homa and the rest. He states the stations of the nyāsa: "aṅgulīṣu." This is the order of the dissolution-nyāsa. Thus: beginning from the little toe of the left foot up to the (great) toe, one should place the pentad beginning with earth — "Oṁ parāya pṛthivyātmane dharāyai namaḥ," and so on. Beginning from the little toe of the right foot up to the great toe, one should place the pentad beginning with smell. On the left and right thighs, in order, manas and ahaṅkāra. On the left and right arms, one should place Avyakta and Mahat. From Puruṣa up to Śakti one should place on the heart. The reverse of this is the creation-order: namely, from Śakti up to Puruṣa on the heart; Avyakta, Mahat, ahaṅkāra and manas in order on the right and left arms and thighs; from the thumb of the right hand up to the little finger one should place the pentad beginning with hearing; from the (thumb of the) left hand up to the little finger one should place the pentad beginning with speech and ending with the generative organ; from the great toe of the right foot to the little toe, the pentad beginning with sound; from the great toe of the left foot to the little toe one should place the pentad beginning with ether — such is the creation-nyāsa. From earth up to Puruṣa is the maintenance-nyāsa. The creation–maintenance and dissolution–maintenance nyāsa belongs to householders; the dissolution–maintenance and creation–dissolution nyāsa belongs to ascetics — this is to be gathered from another āgama. As they say: "This nyāsa, ending with dissolution, is enjoined for the mendicant Vaikhānasas; ending with maintenance, for the householders; ending with creation, for the (celibate) students" — so they declare. Here the purport is only this: that by touching the several limbs with the mantra of the several deities, one comes to have a body purified by the mantras, since it is said "pierced through by the mantra-forms" — not that "those gods reside in him, the ignorant one," for that would conflict with other authorities.
