# Prāyaścittārghya — Yadadya Kacca (morning)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं यदद्य कच्च वृत्रहन्नुदगा अभि सूर्य ।
> सर्वं तदिन्द्र ते वशे । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ yadadya kacca vṛtrahannudagā abhi sūrya ।
> sarvaṃ tadindra te vaśe । oṃ ।

## English meaning

O slayer of Vṛtra (Indra/Sūrya), whatever the Sun has risen over today — all that, O Indra, is in your power.

## Action while reciting

The morning Prāyaścittārghya — an arghya offered in compensation for missing the proper time of arghya; offer one arghya with this mantra. Viniyoga: *yadadya kacceti mantrasya suvākṣa ṛṣiḥ; gāyatrī chandaḥ; sūryo devatā; prātaḥsandhyākālātīta-doṣaparihārārtham prāyaścittārghyapradāne viniyogaḥ.*
