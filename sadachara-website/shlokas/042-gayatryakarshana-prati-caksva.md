# Gāyatryākarṣaṇa — Prati Cakṣva Vi Cakṣva

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं प्रति चक्ष्व वि चक्ष्वेन्द्रश्च सोम जागृतम् ।
> रक्षोभ्यो वधमस्य तमशनिं यातुमद्भ्यः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ prati cakṣva vi cakṣvendraśca soma jāgṛtam ।
> rakṣobhyo vadhamasya tamaśaniṃ yātumadbhyaḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

Look forth, look around! O Indra and Soma, be watchful. Hurl your weapon, the thunderbolt, against the rākṣasas and the sorcerers.

## Action while reciting

The Gāyatryākarṣaṇa — invoking and drawing in the Gāyatrī. Viniyoga: *prati cakṣva vi cakṣveti mantrasya vasiṣṭha ṛṣiḥ; anuṣṭup chandaḥ; rakṣohaṇāvindrāsomau devate; gāyatryākarṣaṇe viniyogaḥ.*
