# Mantrahīnaṃ Kriyāhīnaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं जनार्दन ।
> यत्कृतं तु मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तु ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> mantrahīnaṃ kriyāhīnaṃ bhaktihīnaṃ janārdana ।
> yat kṛtaṃ tu mayā deva paripūrṇaṃ tadastu te ॥

## English meaning

O Janārdana, whatever I have done deficient in mantra, deficient in proper rite, and deficient in devotion — O Lord, may that be (accepted as) complete by You.

## Action while reciting

The kṣamāpaṇa (seeking pardon) verse, recited at the close of worship to ask the Lord to accept the imperfect pūjā as complete.
