# Ekādaśyupavāsena

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> एकादश्युपवासेन द्वादश्यां पारणेन च ।
> यदर्जितं मया पुण्यं तेन प्रीणातु केशवः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ekādaśyupavāsena dvādaśyāṃ pāraṇena ca ।
> yadarjitaṃ mayā puṇyaṃ tena prīṇātu keśavaḥ ॥

## English meaning

By the merit I have earned through fasting on ekādaśī and breaking the fast on dvādaśī — by that, may Keśava be pleased.

## Action while reciting

Recited at the dvādaśī pāraṇa (breaking the ekādaśī fast), offering the merit of the observance to Keśava.
