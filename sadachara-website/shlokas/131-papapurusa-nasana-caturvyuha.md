# Pāpapuruṣa-nāśana (Caturvyūha Bhūtaśuddhi)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं यं (नाभौ) षट्कोणमण्डलमध्यस्थो नीलवर्णो वाय्वन्तर्गतः श्री प्रद्युम्नो भगवान् मच्छरीरस्थं पापपुरुषं वायुना शोषयतु । ओं यं ।
> ओं रं (हृदये) त्रिकोणमण्डलमध्यस्थो रक्तवर्णो वह्न्यन्तर्गतश्रीमदनिरुद्धो भगवान् मच्छरीरस्थं पापपुरुषं वह्निना निर्दहतु । ओं रं ।
> नासाग्रस्थः सङ्कर्षणो भगवान् मच्छरीरस्थितदग्धपापपुरुषस्य भस्म निःसारयतु ।
> ओं वं (शिरसि) वर्तुलमण्डलमध्यस्थः श्वेतवर्णो वरुणान्तर्गतः श्रीवासुदेवो भगवान् मच्छरीरममृतवृष्ट्याऽऽप्लावयतु । ओं वं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ yaṃ (nābhau) ṣaṭkoṇamaṇḍalamadhyastho nīlavarṇo vāyvantargataḥ śrī pradyumno bhagavān maccharīrasthaṃ pāpapuruṣaṃ vāyunā śoṣayatu । oṃ yaṃ ।
> oṃ raṃ (hṛdaye) trikoṇamaṇḍalamadhyastho raktavarṇo vahnyantargataśrīmadaniruddho bhagavān maccharīrasthaṃ pāpapuruṣaṃ vahninā nirdahatu । oṃ raṃ ।
> nāsāgrasthaḥ saṅkarṣaṇo bhagavān maccharīrasthitadagdhapāpapuruṣasya bhasma niḥsārayatu ।
> oṃ vaṃ (śirasi) vartulamaṇḍalamadhyasthaḥ śvetavarṇo varuṇāntargataḥ śrīvāsudevo bhagavān maccharīramamṛtavṛṣṭyā''plāvayatu । oṃ vaṃ ।

## English meaning

Oṃ yaṃ — Lord Pradyumna, dark-blue, seated within a hexagonal maṇḍala at the navel, abiding in the wind — may He dry up, by the wind, the sin-person (pāpapuruṣa) lodged in my body. Oṃ raṃ — Lord Aniruddha, red, seated within a triangular maṇḍala at the heart, abiding in fire — may He burn up, by fire, the sin-person lodged in my body. May Lord Saṅkarṣaṇa, at the tip of the nose, expel the ashes of the now-burnt sin-person lodged in my body. Oṃ vaṃ — Lord Vāsudeva, white, seated within a round maṇḍala at the head, abiding in Varuṇa (the waters) — may He flood my body with a shower of nectar.

## Action while reciting

The Caturvyūha bhūtaśuddhi, continuing the Pāpapuruṣa-dhyāna (shloka 055): the sin-person visualized in the belly is dried by Pradyumna/vāyu (*oṃ yaṃ*, uttered 6 times), burnt by Aniruddha/agni (*oṃ raṃ*, uttered 12 times), its ashes expelled through the left nostril by Saṅkarṣaṇa at the nose-tip, and the body then flooded with nectar by Vāsudeva/Varuṇa (*oṃ vaṃ*, uttered 24 times).
