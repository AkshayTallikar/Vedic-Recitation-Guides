# Bho Svāmin Jagatāṃ Nātha

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> भो स्वामिन् जगतां नाथ यावत्पूजासमाप्तनम् ।
> तावत्सम्प्रीतिभावेन बिम्बेऽस्मिन् संस्थितो भव ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> bho svāmin jagatāṃ nātha yāvatpūjāsamāptanam ।
> tāvatsamprītibhāvena bimbe'smin saṃsthito bhava ॥

## English meaning

O Lord, Master of the worlds — until the worship is completed, so long, with loving favour, remain present in this sacred image.

## Action while reciting

The āvāhana-sthāpana prayer, requesting the Lord (after being invoked from one's heart into the image) to remain present in the icon for the duration of the pūjā.
