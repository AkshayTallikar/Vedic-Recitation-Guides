# Jātaḥ Kaṃsavadhārthāya (Janmāṣṭamī arghya)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> जातः कंसवधार्थाय भूभारहरणाय च ।
> कौरवाणां विनाशाय दैत्यानां निधनाय च ॥
> पाण्डवानां हितार्थाय धर्मसंस्थापनाय च ।
> गृहाणार्घ्यं मया दत्तं देवक्या सहितो हरे ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> jātaḥ kaṃsavadhārthāya bhūbhāraharaṇāya ca ।
> kauravāṇāṃ vināśāya daityānāṃ nidhanāya ca ॥
> pāṇḍavānāṃ hitārthāya dharmasaṃsthāpanāya ca ।
> gṛhāṇārghyaṃ mayā dattaṃ devakyā sahito hare ॥

## English meaning

O Hari, born to slay Kaṃsa and to relieve the earth of its burden; to destroy the Kauravas and to slaughter the demons; for the welfare of the Pāṇḍavas and the re-establishment of dharma — accept this arghya offered by me, together with Devakī.

## Action while reciting

The arghya mantra of **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Janmāṣṭamī**. Chant it and offer arghya — "*devakīsahita śrīkṛṣṇāya idamarghyam*" — pouring water three times. (Worship Kṛṣṇa, Balabhadra, Vasudeva, Devakī, Yaśodā, Nandagopa and Subhadrā; perform archana with bilva-patra.)
