# Ye Ke Cāsmatkule Jātā (Vastra-niṣpīḍana variant)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ये के चास्मत्कुले जाता अपुत्रा गोत्रिणो मृताः ।
> ते गृह्णन्तु मया दत्तं वस्त्रनिष्पीडनोदकम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ye ke cāsmatkule jātā aputrā gotriṇo mṛtāḥ ।
> te gṛhṇantu mayā dattaṃ vastraniṣpīḍanodakam ॥

## English meaning

Whoever in our family have been born and died sonless, of our gotra — may they accept this water, wrung from my (wet) cloth and offered by me.

## Action while reciting

The companion to the previous verse: first shift the tuft of hair to the right and offer the hair-water tarpaṇa to the pitṛ-devatā (Aryamā) and the sonless ancestors, remembering Sri Hari throughout; then, wearing the yajñopavīta like a garland, offer this cloth-water (*vastra-niṣpīḍana*) tarpaṇa.
