# Svatantrāyākhileśāya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> स्वतन्त्रायाखिलेशाय निर्दोषगुणरूपिणे ।
> श्रेयसे मे सुपूर्णाय नमो नारायणाय ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> svatantrāyākhileśāya nirdoṣaguṇarūpiṇe ।
> śreyase me supūrṇāya namo nārāyaṇāya te ॥

## English meaning

To the wholly independent one, the Lord of all, whose very form is flawless excellence; to the utterly complete one, the bestower of my highest good — to You, O Nārāyaṇa, salutations.

## Action while reciting

A salutation to Nārāyaṇa within the closing namaskāra-stuti, praising His independence (svātantrya) and flawless nature.
