# Dhāraṇād Ūrdhvapuṇḍrasya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> धारणाद् ऊर्ध्वपुण्ड्रस्य शरीरं मन्दिरं हरेः ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> dhāraṇād ūrdhvapuṇḍrasya śarīraṃ mandiraṃ hareḥ ।

## English meaning

By wearing the *ūrdhvapuṇḍra* (vertical mark), the body becomes the temple of Hari. The marks (*nāmas*) indicate that the human body belongs to the Lord, is His abode, and is ever under His possession.

## Action while reciting

Perform ācamana and apply gopīcandana with *nirmālyatīrtha*, chanting this mantra.
