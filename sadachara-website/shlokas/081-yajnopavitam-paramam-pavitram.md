# Yajñopavītaṃ Paramaṃ Pavitram

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं यज्ञोपवीतं परमं पवित्रं प्रजापतेर्यत्सहजं पुरस्तात् ।
> आयुष्यमग्र्यं प्रतिमुञ्च शुभ्रं यज्ञोपवीतं बलमस्तु तेजः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ yajñopavītaṃ paramaṃ pavitraṃ prajāpateryatsahajaṃ purastāt ।
> āyuṣyamagryaṃ pratimuñca śubhraṃ yajñopavītaṃ balamastu tejaḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

The sacred thread (yajñopavīta) is supremely pure, born with Prajāpati of old. Put on this bright thread that bestows long life and is foremost; may the yajñopavīta be strength and lustre to me.

## Action while reciting

Recited when wearing the sacred thread (utter three times and put it on); then perform ācamana and japa the Gāyatrī ten times. Viniyoga: *yajñopavītamityasya mantrasya parabrahma ṛṣiḥ; triṣṭup chandaḥ; paramātmā devatā; upavītadhāraṇe viniyogaḥ.*
