# Nārāyaṇāya Paripūrṇaguṇārṇavāya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नारायणाय परिपूर्णगुणार्णवाय विश्वोदयस्थितिलयोनियतिप्रदाय ।
> ज्ञानप्रदाय विबुधासुरसौख्यदुःखसत्कारणाय वितताय नमो नमस्ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> nārāyaṇāya paripūrṇaguṇārṇavāya viśvodayasthitilayoniyatipradāya ।
> jñānapradāya vibudhāsurasaukhyaduḥkhasatkāraṇāya vitatāya namo namaste ॥

## English meaning

To Nārāyaṇa, the ocean of perfect and complete auspicious qualities; the giver of the world's arising, sustenance, dissolution and ordering; the bestower of knowledge; the true cause of happiness to the gods and sorrow to the demons; the all-pervading one — salutations, salutations to You.

## Action while reciting

A dhyāna verse meditating on Nārāyaṇa as the storehouse of perfect attributes and the creator-sustainer-dissolver of the universe, recited before offering salutations to the guru.
