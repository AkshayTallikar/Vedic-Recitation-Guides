# Īṣaddhautaṃ Navaṃ Śvetaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ईषद्धौतं नवं श्वेतं सदशं यन्नधारितम् ।
> अहतं तद्द्विजानीयात् सर्वकर्मसु शोभनम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> īṣaddhautaṃ navaṃ śvetaṃ sadaśaṃ yannadhāritam ।
> ahataṃ taddvijānīyāt sarvakarmasu śobhanam ॥

## English meaning

A cloth lightly washed, new, white, with its fringe (border) intact, and not previously worn — that should be known as *ahata* (unworn), and it is auspicious for all rites.

## Action while reciting

After drying the body, wear the *kaccha* and offer the snāna-karma to the Lord with the dedication mantra. This verse describes the proper (*ahata*) garment to wear for ritual.
