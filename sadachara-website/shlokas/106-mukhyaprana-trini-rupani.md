# Mukhyaprāṇa — Prathamo Hanūmannāma

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं प्रथमो हनूमन्नाम द्वितीयो भीम एव च ।
> पूर्णप्रज्ञस्तृतीयस्तु भगवत्कार्यसाधकः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ prathamo hanūmannāma dvitīyo bhīma eva ca ।
> pūrṇaprajñastṛtīyastu bhagavatkāryasādhakaḥ ॥

## English meaning

The first (incarnation of Mukhyaprāṇa/Vāyu) bore the name Hanumān, the second was indeed Bhīma, and the third is Pūrṇaprajña (Madhvācārya) — the accomplisher of the Lord's work.

## Action while reciting

Recited while offering naivedya to Śrī Mukhyaprāṇa (Vāyu), invoking his three avatāras — Hanumān, Bhīma and Madhvācārya — as the foremost devotee and doer of God's work. (Followed in the text by a verse of the Vāyu Stuti, *pīṭhe ratnopakḷpte…*, chanted while offering tīrtha thrice.)
