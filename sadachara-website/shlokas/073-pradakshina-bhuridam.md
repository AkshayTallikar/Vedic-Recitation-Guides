# Bhūridaṃ Pradakṣiṇam (Pradakṣiṇa)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं भूरिदं प्रदक्षिणम् । ओं भुवरिदं प्रदक्षिणम् ।
> ओं स्वरिदं प्रदक्षिणम् । ओं भूर्भुवः स्वरिदं प्रदक्षिणम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ bhūridaṃ pradakṣiṇam । oṃ bhuvaridaṃ pradakṣiṇam ।
> oṃ svaridaṃ pradakṣiṇam । oṃ bhūrbhuvaḥ svaridaṃ pradakṣiṇam ॥

## English meaning

This circumambulation is for the earth-realm; this for the mid-realm; this for the heaven-realm; this for all the three realms together.

## Action while reciting

Recited while performing the four rounds of pradakṣiṇa (self-circumambulation).
