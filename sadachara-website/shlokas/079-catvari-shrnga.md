# Catvāri Śṛṅgā (Agni / Yajña)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं चत्वारि शृङ्गा त्रयो अस्य पादा द्वे शीर्षे सप्त हस्तासो अस्य ।
> त्रिधा बद्धो वृषभो रोरवीति महो देवो मर्त्यां आ विवेश ॥ ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ catvāri śṛṅgā trayo asya pādā dve śīrṣe sapta hastāso asya ।
> tridhā baddho vṛṣabho roravīti maho devo martyāṃ ā viveśa ॥ oṃ ।

## English meaning

Four are his horns, three his feet, two his heads, and seven his hands; thrice bound, the bull (Agni/Yajña) roars aloud — the great god has entered into mortals.

## Action while reciting

A Vedic mantra used in the Agni / Yajña-puruṣa dhyāna. Viniyoga: *catvāri śṛṅgety asya mantrasya gautamo vāmadeva ṛṣiḥ; triṣṭup chandaḥ; agnirdevatā; agnimūrtidhyāne viniyogaḥ.*
