# Gāyatrī Ṣaḍaṅganyāsa

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं तत्सवितुः हृदयाय नमः ।
> ओं वरेण्यं शिरसे स्वाहा ।
> ओं भर्गो देवस्य शिखायै वषट् ।
> ओं धीमहि कवचाय हुम् ।
> ओं धियो यो नः नेत्राभ्यां वौषट् ।
> ओं प्रचोदयात् अस्त्राय फट् ।
> ओं भूर्भुवः स्वरोम् । (इति दिग्बन्धः)

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ tatsavituḥ hṛdayāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ vareṇyaṃ śirase svāhā ।
> oṃ bhargo devasya śikhāyai vaṣaṭ ।
> oṃ dhīmahi kavacāya hum ।
> oṃ dhiyo yo naḥ netrābhyāṃ vauṣaṭ ।
> oṃ pracodayāt astrāya phaṭ ।
> oṃ bhūrbhuvaḥ svarom । (iti digbandhaḥ)

## English meaning

Assigning the phrases of the Gāyatrī to the six limbs: "tatsavituḥ" to the heart, "vareṇyaṃ" to the head, "bhargo devasya" to the tuft, "dhīmahi" to the armour, "dhiyo yo naḥ" to the eyes, "pracodayāt" to the weapon; and "bhūrbhuvaḥ svarom" for the binding of the directions (digbandha).

## Action while reciting

The Ṣaḍaṅganyāsa preceding the Gāyatrī japa — touching heart, head, tuft, body (armour), eyes, and clapping (weapon), concluding with the digbandha.
