# Ramāpatiṃ Pūrṇaguṇaṃ (Guru-paramparā vandana)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> रमापतिं पूर्णगुणं मुकुन्दं व्यासं च विज्ञानसहस्रभानुम् ।
> पूर्णप्रबोधं च सुतत्त्वदीपं क्रमाद्गुरूंश्च प्रणमामि मूर्ध्ना ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ramāpatiṃ pūrṇaguṇaṃ mukundaṃ vyāsaṃ ca vijñānasahasrabhānum ।
> pūrṇaprabodhaṃ ca sutattvadīpaṃ kramādgurūṃśca praṇamāmi mūrdhnā ॥

## English meaning

The Lord of Ramā (Lakṣmī), full of all qualities, Mukunda; Vyāsa, the thousand-rayed sun of wisdom; Pūrṇaprabodha and Sutattvadīpa (the gurus) — to these preceptors in succession I bow with my head.

## Action while reciting

The guru-paramparā vandana of the Hariguru-dhyāna, saluting the line of teachers in order. Followed by "*sarvābhyastattvadevatābhyo namaḥ; sarvābhyo mātṛkādevatābhyo namaḥ*" (salutations to all the tattva-devatās and mātṛkā-devatās).
