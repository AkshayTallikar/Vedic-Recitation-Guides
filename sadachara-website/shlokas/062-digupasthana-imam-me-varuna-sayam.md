# Digupasthāna — Imaṃ Me Varuṇa (evening)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं इमं मे वरुण श्रुधी हवमद्या च मृळय ।
> त्वामवस्युरा चके ॥
> तत्त्वा यामि ब्रह्मणा वन्दमानस्तदा शास्ते यजमानो हविर्भिः ।
> अहेळमानो वरुणेह बोध्युरुशंस मा न आयुः प्रमोषीः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ imaṃ me varuṇa śrudhī havamadyā ca mṛḻaya ।
> tvāmavasyurā cake ॥
> tattvā yāmi brahmaṇā vandamānastadā śāste yajamāno havirbhiḥ ।
> aheḻamāno varuṇeha bodhyuruśaṃsa mā na āyuḥ pramoṣīḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

Hear, O Varuṇa, this call of mine today, and be gracious; longing for your aid I yearn for you. I approach you with sacred prayer, revering you; the worshipper entreats you with oblations. Unangered, O widely-praised Varuṇa, be mindful of us here; do not cut short our span of life.

## Action while reciting

The evening Digupasthāna (worship of the directions / the setting Sun as Varuṇa), standing facing west. It is a prayer to the presiding deities of all the directions. Viniyoga: *ājīgartiḥ śunaḥśepa ṛṣiḥ; tatastriṣṭup ādyā gāyatrī chandasī; varuṇo devatā.*
