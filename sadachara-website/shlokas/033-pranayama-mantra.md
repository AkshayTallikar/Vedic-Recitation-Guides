# Prāṇāyāma Mantra (Vyāhṛti–Gāyatrī)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं भूः । ओं भुवः । ओं स्वः । ओं महः । ओं जनः । ओं तपः । ओं सत्यम् ।
> ओं तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि ।
> धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ।
> ओमापो ज्योतीरसोऽमृतं ब्रह्म भूर्भुवःस्वरोम् ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ bhūḥ । oṃ bhuvaḥ । oṃ svaḥ । oṃ mahaḥ । oṃ janaḥ । oṃ tapaḥ । oṃ satyam ।
> oṃ tatsaviturvareṇyaṃ bhargo devasya dhīmahi ।
> dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt ।
> omāpo jyotīraso'mṛtaṃ brahma bhūrbhuvaḥsvarom ।

## English meaning

Oṃ — earth, atmosphere, heaven, the great, the created, the austere, the true (the seven realms). We meditate upon that adorable splendour of the divine Savitṛ; may He inspire our intellects. Oṃ — the waters, the light, the essence (rasa), the immortal Brahman, the earth-atmosphere-heaven — Oṃ.

## Action while reciting

The Prāṇāyāma mantra, preceded by its viniyoga (*praṇavasya parabrahma ṛṣiḥ; daivī gāyatrī chandaḥ; paramātmā devatā; prāṇāyāme viniyogaḥ*). Hold the nose with the thumb and ring finger of the right hand; exhale through the right nostril, then perform controlled breathing (pūraka, kumbhaka, recaka) while mentally reciting this mantra.
