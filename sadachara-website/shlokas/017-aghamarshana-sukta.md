# Aghamarṣaṇa Sūkta (Ṛtaṃ ca satyaṃ)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं ऋतं च सत्यं चाभीद्धात् तपसोऽध्यजायत ।
> ततो रात्र्यजायत ततः समुद्रो अर्णवः ॥
> समुद्रादर्णवादधि संवत्सरो अजायत ।
> अहोरात्राणि विदधद् विश्वस्य मिषतो वशी ॥
> सूर्याचन्द्रमसौ धाता यथापूर्वमकल्पयत् ।
> दिवं च पृथिवीं चान्तरिक्षमथो स्वः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ ṛtaṃ ca satyaṃ cābhīddhāt tapaso'dhyajāyata ।
> tato rātryajāyata tataḥ samudro arṇavaḥ ॥
> samudrādarṇavādadhi saṃvatsaro ajāyata ।
> ahorātrāṇi vidadhad viśvasya miṣato vaśī ॥
> sūryācandramasau dhātā yathāpūrvamakalpayat ।
> divaṃ ca pṛthivīṃ cāntarikṣamatho svaḥ ॥

## English meaning

From the kindled tapas (austerity) were born *ṛta* (cosmic order) and *satya* (truth); thence was born night, thence the surging ocean. From the surging ocean was born the year, ordaining days and nights, the controller of all that blinks (lives). The Creator (Dhātṛ) fashioned the sun and the moon as before, and the heaven, the earth, the mid-region, and the realm of light.

## Action while reciting

The *Aghamarṣaṇa* (sin-effacing) sūkta — *ṛṣi*: Aghamarṣaṇa; *chandas*: Anuṣṭup; *devatā*: Nārāyaṇa the creator. Recited while standing in the bathing water.
