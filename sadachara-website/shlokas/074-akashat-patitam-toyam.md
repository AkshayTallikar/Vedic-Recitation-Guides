# Ākāśāt Patitaṃ Toyaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> आकाशात् पतितं तोयं यथा गच्छति सागरम् ।
> सर्वदेवनमस्कारः केशवं प्रति गच्छति ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ākāśāt patitaṃ toyaṃ yathā gacchati sāgaram ।
> sarvadevanamaskāraḥ keśavaṃ prati gacchati ॥

## English meaning

Just as water fallen from the sky finds its way to the ocean, so does salutation offered to any of the gods reach Keśava (Viṣṇu).

## Action while reciting

Recited as the universal salutation (sarvadevanamaskāra) — affirming that all worship ultimately reaches the Supreme Lord. (In the morning facing east, in the evening facing west.)
