# Mātarme Mātariśvan (Hari Vāyu Stuti)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> मातर्मे मातरिश्वन् पितरतुलगुरो भ्रातरिष्टाप्तबन्धो
> स्वामिन् सर्वान्तरात्मन्नजर जरयितर् जन्ममृत्यामयानाम् ।
> गोविन्दे देहि भक्तिं भवति च भगवन्नूर्जितां निर्निमित्तां
> निर्व्याजां निश्चलां सद्गुणगणबृहतीं शाश्वतीमाशु देव ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> mātarme mātariśvan pitaratulaguro bhrātariṣṭāptabandho
> svāmin sarvāntarātmann ajara jarayitar janmamṛtyāmayānām ।
> govinde dehi bhaktiṃ bhavati ca bhagavann ūrjitāṃ nirnimittāṃ
> nirvyājāṃ niścalāṃ sadguṇagaṇabṛhatīṃ śāśvatīmāśu deva ॥

## English meaning

O my Mother, O Mātariśvan (Vāyu, Mukhyaprāṇa); O Father; O peerless Guru; O Brother; O dear and trusted Kinsman; O Master; O inner Self of all; O ageless one; O destroyer of the ailments of birth and death — grant me devotion to Govinda, and let it be, O Lord, ever-ascendant and strong, free of any motive, without guile, unwavering, vast with the host of good qualities, and eternal — grant it swiftly, O God.

## Action while reciting

The book's concluding verse of the namaskāra-stuti — this is **verse 14 of the Hari Vāyu Stuti** of Trivikrama Paṇḍitācārya, addressing Mukhyaprāṇa (Vāyu/Mātariśvan) as mother, father, guru, brother and kinsman, and praying through him for pure, causeless, unwavering, eternal devotion to Govinda.
