# Paramātmane Satatamekarūpiṇe

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> परमात्मने सततमेकरूपिणे दशरूपिणे शतसहस्ररूपिणे ।
> अविकारिणे स्फुटमनन्तरूपिणे सुखचित्समस्ततनवे नमो नमः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> paramātmane satatamekarūpiṇe daśarūpiṇe śatasahasrarūpiṇe ।
> avikāriṇe sphuṭamanantarūpiṇe sukhacitsamastatanave namo namaḥ ॥

## English meaning

To the Supreme Self — ever one in form, yet of ten forms and of a hundred-thousand forms; to the changeless one whose forms are manifestly infinite, whose whole body is bliss-and-consciousness — salutations, salutations.

## Action while reciting

A salutation within the closing namaskāra-stuti, praising the Supreme Self as simultaneously one and of countless forms, His very being made of consciousness and bliss (sukha-cit).
