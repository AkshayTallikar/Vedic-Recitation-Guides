# Devī Sūkta — Ahaṃ Rudrebhiḥ (opening verses)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> अहं रुद्रेभिर्वसुभिश्चराम्यहमादित्यैरुत विश्वदेवैः ।
> अहं मित्रावरुणोभा बिभर्म्यहमिन्द्राग्नी अहमश्विनोभा ॥
> अहं सोममाहनसं बिभर्म्यहं त्वष्टारमुत पूषणं भगम् ।
> अहं दधामि द्रविणं हविष्मते सुप्राव्ये यजमानाय सुन्वते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ahaṃ rudrebhirvasubhiścarāmyahamādityairuta viśvadevaiḥ ।
> ahaṃ mitrāvaruṇobhā bibharmyahamindrāgnī ahamaśvinobhā ॥
> ahaṃ somamāhanasaṃ bibharmyahaṃ tvaṣṭāramuta pūṣaṇaṃ bhagam ।
> ahaṃ dadhāmi draviṇaṃ haviṣmate suprāvye yajamānāya sunvate ॥

## English meaning

I move with the Rudras and the Vasus, with the Ādityas and the All-Gods; I uphold both Mitra and Varuṇa, both Indra and Agni, both the Aśvins. I bear the swelling Soma, and Tvaṣṭṛ, Pūṣan and Bhaga; I bestow wealth on the devout sacrificer who presses Soma and offers oblation.

## Action while reciting

The opening of the **Devī Sūkta** (Ambhṛṇī Sūkta, RV 10.125), in which the Goddess (Vāk / Mahālakṣmī) declares her all-pervading sovereignty. Recited in Devī worship. Viniyoga: *ahaṃ rudreti mantrasya lakṣmī ṛṣiḥ; triṣṭup (dvitīya jagatī) chandaḥ; mahālakṣmīr devatā; abhiṣeke viniyogaḥ.* (Continues for eight verses.)
