# Apavitraḥ Pavitro Vā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
> यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṃ gato'pi vā ।
> yaḥ smaret puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ ॥

## English meaning

Whether impure or pure, or in whatever condition one may be — whoever remembers the lotus-eyed Lord (Viṣṇu) becomes pure within and without.

## Action while reciting

The pavitrīkaraṇa verse, recited (sprinkling water with tulasī) to purify oneself and the articles of worship before the pūjā.
