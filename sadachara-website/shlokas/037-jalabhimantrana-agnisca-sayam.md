# Jalābhimantraṇa — Agniśca (evening)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं अग्निश्च मा मन्युश्च मन्युपतयश्च मन्युकृतेभ्यः ।
> पापेभ्यो रक्षन्ताम् ।
> यदह्ना पापमकार्षम् ।
> मनसा वाचा हस्ताभ्याम् ।
> पद्भ्यामुदरेण शिश्ना ।
> अहस्तदवलुम्पतु ।
> यत्किञ्च दुरितं मयि ।
> इदमहं माममृतयोनौ ।
> सत्ये ज्योतिषि जुहोमि स्वाहा । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ agniśca mā manyuśca manyupatayaśca manyukṛtebhyaḥ ।
> pāpebhyo rakṣantām ।
> yadahnā pāpamakārṣam ।
> manasā vācā hastābhyām ।
> padbhyāmudareṇa śiśnā ।
> ahastadavalumpatu ।
> yatkiñca duritaṃ mayi ।
> idamahaṃ māmamṛtayonau ।
> satye jyotiṣi juhomi svāhā । oṃ ।

## English meaning

May Agni, and Manyu (wrath), and the lords of wrath, protect me from the sins arising from anger. Whatever sin I have committed during the day — by mind, speech, hands, feet, belly, or organs — may the day wipe it away. Whatever evil there is in me, I offer it into the true light, the immortal source — svāhā.

## Action while reciting

The evening Jalābhimantraṇa (counterpart of the morning *sūryaśca*): drink the water on *svāhā*. Then do ācamana twice (*dvirācamya*) and repeat the Mārjana with the *āpohiṣṭhā* mantra. Viniyoga: *…prakṛtiḥ chandaḥ; agnirdevatā; jalābhimantraṇe viniyogaḥ.*
