# Haṃsaḥ Śuciṣad (first noon arghya)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं हंसः शुचिषद्वसुरन्तरिक्षसद्धोता वेदिषदतिथिर्दुरोणसत् ।
> नृषद्वरसदृतसद्व्योमसदब्जा गोजा ऋतजा अद्रिजा ऋतम् । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ haṃsaḥ śuciṣadvasurantarikṣasaddhotā vediṣadatithirduroṇasat ।
> nṛṣadvarasadṛtasadvyomasadabjā gojā ṛtajā adrijā ṛtam । oṃ ।

## English meaning

He (the sun, the haṃsa) sits in radiant purity; the Vasu, he sits in mid-air; the Hotṛ, he sits at the altar; the guest, he sits in the house. Sitting among men, sitting in the choicest place, sitting in the true, sitting in the sky — born of the waters, born of light, born of ṛta, born of the mountain — he is the truth.

## Action while reciting

The first of the three madhyāhna sūryārghya mantras (Ṛgvedic). The three arghyas of the noon sandhyā are offered with (1) this *haṃsaḥ śuciṣad* verse, (2) *ā kṛṣṇena rajasā* (shloka 038), and (3) the Gāyatrī mantra. Viniyoga: *haṃsaḥ śuciṣadityasya mantrasya gautamo vāmadeva ṛṣiḥ; jagatī chandaḥ; sūryo devatā; mādhyāhnikasandhyāṅgasūryārghyapradāne viniyogaḥ.*
