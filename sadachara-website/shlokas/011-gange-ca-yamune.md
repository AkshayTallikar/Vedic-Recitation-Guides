# Gaṅge Ca Yamune (Seven Rivers Invocation)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> गङ्गे च यमुने चैव गोदावरि सरस्वति ।
> नर्मदे सिन्धु कावेरि जलेऽस्मिन् सन्निधिं कुरु ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> gaṅge ca yamune caiva godāvari sarasvati ।
> narmade sindhu kāveri jale'smin sannidhiṃ kuru ॥

## English meaning

O Gaṅgā and Yamunā, O Godāvarī, Sarasvatī, Narmadā, Sindhu and Kāverī — be present in this water.

## Action while reciting

After the dip, perform ācamana, then the saṅkalpa: invoke the seven sacred rivers into the bathing water with this verse. (The full deśa-kāla saṅkalpa formula — "govinda govinda… adya brahmaṇaḥ dvitīya-parārdhe…" — follows in the book as a prose declaration of place, time and intent.)
