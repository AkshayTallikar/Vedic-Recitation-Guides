# Ehi Sūrya Sahasrāṃśo (Arghya to Sūrya)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> एहि सूर्य सहस्रांशो तेजोराशे जगत्पते ।
> अनुकम्पय मां नित्यं गृहाणार्घ्यं नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ehi sūrya sahasrāṃśo tejorāśe jagatpate ।
> anukampaya māṃ nityaṃ gṛhāṇārghyaṃ namo'stu te ॥

## English meaning

Come, O Sūrya of a thousand rays, O mass of splendour, O Lord of the world; ever show me compassion — accept this arghya, salutations to you.

## Action while reciting

Chanting this mantra, offer arghya to Sūryadeva.
