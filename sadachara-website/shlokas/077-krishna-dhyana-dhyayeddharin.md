# Kṛṣṇa Dhyāna — Dhyāyeddharinmaṇinibhaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ध्यायेद्धरिन्मणिनिभं जगदेकवन्द्यं
> सौन्दर्यसारमरिशङ्खवराभयानि ।
> दोर्भिर्दधानमजितं सरसं च भैष्मी-
> सत्यासमेतमखिलप्रदमिन्दिरेशम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> dhyāyeddharinmaṇinibhaṃ jagadekavandyaṃ
> saundaryasāramariśaṅkhavarābhayāni ।
> dorbhirdadhānamajitaṃ sarasaṃ ca bhaiṣmī-
> satyāsametamakhilapradamindireśam ॥

## English meaning

One should meditate on the unconquered Kṛṣṇa, hued like a blue sapphire, the sole adored of the world, the very essence of beauty, bearing in His arms the discus, conch, boon-gesture and abhaya; the charming one, together with Bhaiṣmī (Rukmiṇī) and Satyā (Satyabhāmā), the giver of all, the Lord of Indirā (Lakṣmī).

## Action while reciting

The dhyāna recited before the Kṛṣṇa mantra japa.
