# Indra Śreṣṭhāni Draviṇāni (East)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं इन्द्र श्रेष्ठानि द्रविणानि धेहि चित्तिं दक्षस्य सुभगत्वमस्मे ।
> पोषं रयीणामरिष्टिं तनूनां स्वाद्मानं वाचः सुदिनत्वमह्नाम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ indra śreṣṭhāni draviṇāni dhehi cittiṃ dakṣasya subhagatvamasme ।
> poṣaṃ rayīṇāmariṣṭiṃ tanūnāṃ svādmānaṃ vācaḥ sudinatvamahnām ॥

## English meaning

O Indra, grant us the best of treasures, an able mind, and good fortune; growth of wealth, health of body, sweetness of speech, and fair days.

## Action while reciting

The first of the four direction-facing prayers — recited facing east (in the morning starting from the east, in the evening from the west), with folded hands. Viniyoga: *indra śreṣṭhānīti gṛtsamadaḥ; indras triṣṭup.*
