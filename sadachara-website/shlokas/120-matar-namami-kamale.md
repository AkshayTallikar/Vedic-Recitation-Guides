# Mātar Namāmi Kamale (Lakṣmī)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> मातर्नमामि कमले कमलासनाद्यैर्देवैः पदाम्बुजयुगं तव सेन्द्ररुद्रैः ।
> आराधितं विविधभूरुहदिव्यपुष्पैर्मां रक्ष रक्ष वितरस्व ममेप्सितार्थान् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> mātarnamāmi kamale kamalāsanādyairdevaiḥ padāmbujayugaṃ tava sendrarudraiḥ ।
> ārādhitaṃ vividhabhūruhadivyapuṣpairmāṃ rakṣa rakṣa vitarasva mamepsitārthān ॥

## English meaning

O Mother, O lotus-dweller (Kamalā/Lakṣmī), I bow to Your two lotus-feet — worshipped by Brahmā (the lotus-seated) and the other gods, along with Indra and Rudra, with the divine flowers of many trees. Protect me, protect me, and grant me my desired ends.

## Action while reciting

A prayer to Lakṣmī within the closing namaskāra-stuti, bowing to her lotus-feet (themselves worshipped by Brahmā, Indra, Rudra and the gods) and asking her protection and the fulfilment of one's wishes.
