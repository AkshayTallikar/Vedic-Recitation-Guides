# Yamāya Dharmarājāya (South — 14 names of Yama)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं यमाय धर्मराजाय मृत्यवे चान्तकाय च ।
> वैवस्वताय कालाय सर्वभूतक्षयाय च ॥
> औदुम्बराय दध्नाय नीलाय परमेष्ठिने ।
> वृकोदराय चित्राय चित्रगुप्ताय वै नमः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ yamāya dharmarājāya mṛtyave cāntakāya ca ।
> vaivasvatāya kālāya sarvabhūtakṣayāya ca ॥
> audumbarāya dadhnāya nīlāya parameṣṭhine ।
> vṛkodarāya citrāya citraguptāya vai namaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to Yama, to Dharmarāja, to Mṛtyu (Death), to Antaka, to Vaivasvata, to Kāla (Time), to the destroyer of all beings, to Audumbara, to Dadhna, to Nīla, to Parameṣṭhin, to Vṛkodara, to Citra, and to Citragupta — the fourteen names of Yama.

## Action while reciting

The second of the four direction-facing prayers — recited facing south.
