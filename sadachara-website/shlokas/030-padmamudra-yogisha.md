# Padmamudrā — Yogīśa

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> संसारभयभीतानां योगिनामभयप्रदः ।
> पद्महस्तेन यो देवो योगीशं तं नमाम्यहम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> saṃsārabhayabhītānāṃ yoginām abhayapradaḥ ।
> padmahastena yo devo yogīśaṃ taṃ namāmyaham ॥

## English meaning

The Lord who gives fearlessness to the yogis frightened by the fear of saṃsāra, who bears the lotus in His hand — that Lord of yogis I bow to.

## Action while reciting

Recited while wearing the **Padmamudrā** (lotus mark) on the middle of the body. The five mudrās are: Cakra, Śaṅkha, Gadā, Padma and Nārāyaṇa.
