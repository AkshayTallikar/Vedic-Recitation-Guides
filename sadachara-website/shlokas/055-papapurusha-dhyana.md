# Pāpapuruṣa-dhyāna (Brahmahatyāśiraskaṃ)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ब्रह्महत्याशिरस्कं च स्वर्णस्तेयभुजद्वयम् ।
> सुरापानहृदायुक्तं गुरुतल्पकटिद्वयम् ॥
> तत्संयोगपदद्वन्द्वमङ्गप्रत्यङ्गपातकम् ।
> उपपातकरोमाणं रक्तश्मश्रुविलोचनम् ॥
> खड्गचर्मधरं कृष्णं कुक्षौ पापं विचिन्तयेत् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> brahmahatyāśiraskaṃ ca svarṇasteyabhujadvayam ।
> surāpānahṛdāyuktaṃ gurutalpakaṭidvayam ॥
> tatsaṃyogapadadvandvamaṅgapratyaṅgapātakam ।
> upapātakaromāṇaṃ raktaśmaśruvilocanam ॥
> khaḍgacarmadharaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ kukṣau pāpaṃ vicintayet ॥

## English meaning

One should visualize sin personified, dwelling in the belly: with the killing of a brahmin for its head, the stealing of gold for its two arms, the drinking of liquor for its heart, violating the guru's bed for its two hips, their combination for its two feet, the major sins for its limbs and minor limbs, the lesser sins for its body-hair — red-bearded and red-eyed, dark, bearing sword and shield.

## Action while reciting

The Pāpapuruṣa-dhyāna — visualizing the personified sin in the left side of the belly so as to cast it out (drawing it then to the navel), as part of inner purification before the Gāyatrī japa.
