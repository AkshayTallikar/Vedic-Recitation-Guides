# Kāyena Vācā Manasendriyairvā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा बुद्ध्यात्मना वा नुसृष्टभावैः ।
> करोमि यद्यत्सकलं परस्मै नारायणायेति समर्पयामि ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> kāyena vācā manasendriyairvā buddhyātmanā vā nusṛṣṭabhāvaiḥ ।
> karomi yadyatsakalaṃ parasmai nārāyaṇāyeti samarpayāmi ॥

## English meaning

Whatever I do — by body, speech, mind or senses, by intellect or soul, or by the promptings of (my) nature — all of it I offer up to the supreme Nārāyaṇa.

## Action while reciting

The sarva-samarpaṇa verse, recited at the end of the pūjā (and of any action), surrendering all of one's deeds to Nārāyaṇa.
