# Namo Namo Namo Namo Nato'smi

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नमो नमो नमो नमो नतोऽस्मि ते सदा पदम् ।
> समस्तसद्गुणोच्छ्रितं नमामि ते पदं पुनः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> namo namo namo namo nato'smi te sadā padam ।
> samastasadguṇocchritaṃ namāmi te padaṃ punaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Salutations, salutations, salutations, salutations — I bow ever to Your feet. Again I bow to Your feet, exalted by the whole host of perfect qualities.

## Action while reciting

Part of the namaskāra-stuti recited at the close of worship — a repeated salutation to the Lord's feet.
