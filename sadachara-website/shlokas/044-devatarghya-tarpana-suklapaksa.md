# Devatārghya — Dvādaśanāma Tarpaṇa (bright half)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं केशवं तर्पयामि । ओं नारायणं तर्पयामि । ओं माधवं तर्पयामि ।
> ओं गोविन्दं तर्पयामि । ओं विष्णुं तर्पयामि । ओं मधुसूदनं तर्पयामि ।
> ओं त्रिविक्रमं तर्पयामि । ओं वामनं तर्पयामि । ओं श्रीधरं तर्पयामि ।
> ओं हृषीकेशं तर्पयामि । ओं पद्मनाभं तर्पयामि । ओं दामोदरं तर्पयामि ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ keśavaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ nārāyaṇaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ mādhavaṃ tarpayāmi ।
> oṃ govindaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ viṣṇuṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ madhusūdanaṃ tarpayāmi ।
> oṃ trivikramaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ vāmanaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ śrīdharaṃ tarpayāmi ।
> oṃ hṛṣīkeśaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ padmanābhaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ dāmodaraṃ tarpayāmi ।

## English meaning

I offer tarpaṇa (water-oblation) to Keśava, Nārāyaṇa, Mādhava, Govinda, Viṣṇu, Madhusūdana, Trivikrama, Vāmana, Śrīdhara, Hṛṣīkeśa, Padmanābha, and Dāmodara — the twelve names of the Lord.

## Action while reciting

The Devatārghya offered during the **śuklapakṣa** (bright half of the month), in this forward order of the twelve names. Then perform ācamana twice (*dvirācamya*).
