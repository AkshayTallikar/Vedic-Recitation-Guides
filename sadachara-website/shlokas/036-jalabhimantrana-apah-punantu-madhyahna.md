# Jalābhimantraṇa — Āpaḥ Punantu (midday)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं आपः पुनन्तु पृथिवीं पृथिवी पूता पुनातु माम् ।
> पुनन्तु ब्रह्मणस्पतिर्ब्रह्म पूता पुनातु माम् ।
> यदुच्छिष्टमभोज्यं यद्वा दुश्चरितं मम ।
> सर्वे पुनन्तु मामापोऽसतां च प्रतिग्रह स्वाहा । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ āpaḥ punantu pṛthivīṃ pṛthivī pūtā punātu mām ।
> punantu brahmaṇaspatirbrahma pūtā punātu mām ।
> yaducchiṣṭamabhojyaṃ yadvā duścaritaṃ mama ।
> sarve punantu māmāpo'satāṃ ca pratigraha svāhā । oṃ ।

## English meaning

May the Waters purify the earth; may the purified earth purify me. May Bṛhaspati (Brahmaṇaspati) purify me; may the purified Brahman purify me. Whatever remnant (ucchiṣṭa) is unfit to eat, or whatever misconduct of mine — may the Waters purify me of it all, and of the acceptance of gifts from the unworthy — svāhā.

## Action while reciting

The midday (madhyāhna) Jalābhimantraṇa: hold water in the right palm and drink it on *svāhā* (*iti jalaṃ prāśya*). Viniyoga: *āpaḥ punantvityasya mantrasya pūta ṛṣiḥ; aṣṭī chandaḥ; āpo devatā; apāṃ prāśane viniyogaḥ.*
