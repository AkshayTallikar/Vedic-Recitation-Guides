# Diṅnamaskāra (Salutations to the directions)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं इन्द्राय नमः । ओं अग्नये नमः । ओं यमाय नमः । ओं निर्ऋतये नमः ।
> ओं वरुणाय नमः । ओं वायवे नमः । ओं सोमाय नमः । ओं ईशानाय नमः ।
> ओं सन्ध्यायै नमः । ओं सन्ध्यापतये नमः । ओं गायत्र्यै नमः ।
> ओं सावित्र्यै नमः । ओं सरस्वत्यै नमः । ओं ब्रह्मणे नमः ।
> ओं अनन्ताय नमः । ओं नमो ध्रुवासि ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ indrāya namaḥ । oṃ agnaye namaḥ । oṃ yamāya namaḥ । oṃ nirṛtaye namaḥ ।
> oṃ varuṇāya namaḥ । oṃ vāyave namaḥ । oṃ somāya namaḥ । oṃ īśānāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ sandhyāyai namaḥ । oṃ sandhyāpataye namaḥ । oṃ gāyatryai namaḥ ।
> oṃ sāvitryai namaḥ । oṃ sarasvatyai namaḥ । oṃ brahmaṇe namaḥ ।
> oṃ anantāya namaḥ । oṃ namo dhruvāsi ।

## English meaning

Salutations to Indra (east), Agni (south-east), Yama (south), Nirṛti (south-west), Varuṇa (west), Vāyu (north-west), Soma (north), Īśāna (north-east); to Sandhyā, the Lord of Sandhyā, Gāyatrī, Sāvitrī, Sarasvatī, and Brahmā; to Ananta (looking up); and "salutation, you are Dhruva, the firm one" (looking down).

## Action while reciting

The Diṅnamaskāra — saluting the presiding deities of the directions, turning from east clockwise through south-east, south, and so on; then looking up (to Ananta) and down (to Dhruva).
