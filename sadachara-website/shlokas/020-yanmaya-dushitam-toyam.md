# Yanmayā Dūṣitaṃ Toyaṃ (Yakṣma tarpaṇa)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> यन्मया दूषितं तोयं शरीरमलसम्भवम् ।
> तत्पापशोधनार्थाय यक्ष्माणं तर्पयाम्यहम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> yanmayā dūṣitaṃ toyaṃ śarīramalasambhavam ।
> tatpāpaśodhanārthāya yakṣmāṇaṃ tarpayāmyaham ॥

## English meaning

Whatever water I have polluted with the dirt born of my body — to cleanse that sin, I offer tarpaṇa to Yakṣma.

## Action while reciting

Since bathing in the rivers washes off bodily dirt and so pollutes the holy waters, as expiation offer tarpaṇa once (*yakṣma tarpaṇa*) to any block of stone nearby, chanting this mantra. Conclude the bath with the dedication: "*anena puṇyatīrthasnānena bhagavān … śrī Gopālakṛṣṇaḥ prīyatām*" (may Lord Gopālakṛṣṇa be pleased by this bathing in the holy waters).
