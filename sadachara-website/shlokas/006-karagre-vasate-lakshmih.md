# Karāgre Vasate Lakṣmīḥ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> कराग्रे वसते लक्ष्मीः करमध्ये सरस्वती ।
> करमूले सदा गौरी प्रभाते करदर्शनम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> karāgre vasate lakṣmīḥ karamadhye sarasvatī ।
> karamūle sadā gaurī prabhāte karadarśanam ॥

## English meaning

At the tips of the hands dwells Lakṣmī, in the middle Sarasvatī, and at the base (where it joins the wrist) Gaurī (Pārvatī); therefore in the morning one should look upon one's hands.

## Action while reciting

On waking, spread the palms, join them, and meditate on Lakṣmī at the fingertips, Sarasvatī in the middle, and Gaurī at the base of the palms, then look at the hands. Afterwards: cleanse the body, brush the teeth, wash the face, water the tulasī plant, and circumambulate while chanting.
