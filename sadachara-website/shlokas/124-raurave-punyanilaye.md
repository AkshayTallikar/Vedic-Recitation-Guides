# Raurave Puṇyanilaye

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> रौरवे पुण्यनिलये पद्मार्बुदनिवासिनाम् ।
> अर्थिनामुदकं दत्तमक्षय्यमुपतिष्ठतु ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> raurave puṇyanilaye padmārbudanivāsinām ।
> arthināmudakaṃ dattamakṣayyamupatiṣṭhatu ॥

## English meaning

For the supplicant beings dwelling in Raurava, in the realms of merit, and in (the regions called) Padma and Arbuda — may the water given (by me) abide inexhaustibly.

## Action while reciting

Recited during the meal while setting aside a little water/food, offering it for the sustenance of beings dwelling in all the worlds.
