# Aṣṭapuṣpa — Ahiṃsā Prathamaṃ Puṣpaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> अहिंसा प्रथमं पुष्पं पुष्पमिन्द्रियनिग्रहः ।
> सर्वभूतदयापुष्पं सत्यपुष्पं विशेषतः ॥
> ज्ञानपुष्पं तपःपुष्पं क्रियापुष्पं तथैव च ।
> ध्यानं चैवाष्टमं पुष्पं पुष्पैस्तुष्यति केशवः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ahiṃsā prathamaṃ puṣpaṃ puṣpamindriyanigrahaḥ ।
> sarvabhūtadayāpuṣpaṃ satyapuṣpaṃ viśeṣataḥ ॥
> jñānapuṣpaṃ tapaḥpuṣpaṃ kriyāpuṣpaṃ tathaiva ca ।
> dhyānaṃ caivāṣṭamaṃ puṣpaṃ puṣpaistuṣyati keśavaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Non-violence is the first flower; restraint of the senses a flower; compassion to all beings a flower; truth, above all, a flower. Knowledge a flower, austerity a flower, right action likewise a flower, and meditation the eighth flower — with these flowers Keśava is pleased.

## Action while reciting

Recited as the prārthanā (prayer) at the start of worship, declaring that the truest offering to the Lord is inner virtue — the 'eight flowers' — rather than mere material flowers.
