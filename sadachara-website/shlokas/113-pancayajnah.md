# Pañcayajñāḥ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ब्रह्मयज्ञो देवयज्ञः पितृयज्ञस्तथैव च ।
> भूतयज्ञो नृयज्ञश्च पञ्चयज्ञाः प्रकीर्तिताः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> brahmayajño devayajñaḥ pitṛyajñastathaiva ca ।
> bhūtayajño nṛyajñaśca pañcayajñāḥ prakīrtitāḥ ॥

## English meaning

The sacrifice to Brahman (Veda-study), the sacrifice to the gods, the sacrifice to the ancestors, the sacrifice to beings, and the sacrifice to human guests — these are proclaimed as the five (daily) sacrifices.

## Action while reciting

The verse enumerating the pañcayajñas — the five great daily sacrifices a householder owes and repays each day (study, offering to the gods, to the ancestors, to creatures, and hospitality to guests).
