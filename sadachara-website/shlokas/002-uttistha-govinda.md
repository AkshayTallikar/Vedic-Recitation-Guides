# Uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha Govinda

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> उत्तिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ गोविन्द उत्तिष्ठ गरुडध्वज ।
> उत्तिष्ठ कमलाकान्त त्रैलोक्यं मङ्गलं कुरु ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha govinda uttiṣṭha garuḍadhvaja ।
> uttiṣṭha kamalākānta trailokyaṃ maṅgalaṃ kuru ॥

## English meaning

Arise, arise, O Govinda; arise, O you whose banner bears Garuḍa; arise, O beloved of Kamalā (Lakṣmī), and bring auspiciousness to the three worlds.

## Action while reciting

Recite on waking. Then chant **'Nārāyaṇa' 108 times or 16 times.**
