# Śaṅkhamudrā — Pāñcajanya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> पाञ्चजन्य निजध्वानध्वस्तपातकसञ्चय ।
> त्राहि मां पापिनं घोरसंसारार्णवपातिनम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> pāñcajanya nijadhvānadhvastapātakasañcaya ।
> trāhi māṃ pāpinaṃ ghorasaṃsārārṇavapātinam ॥

## English meaning

O Pāñcajanya (conch), by whose own sound the accumulated heap of sins is destroyed — save me, a sinner fallen into the terrible ocean of saṃsāra.

## Action while reciting

Recited while wearing the **Śaṅkhamudrā** (conch mark) on the left side of the body.
