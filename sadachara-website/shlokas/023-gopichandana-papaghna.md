# Gopīcandana Pāpaghna

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> गोपीचन्दन पापघ्न विष्णुदेहसमुद्भव ।
> चक्राङ्कित नमस्तेऽस्तु धारणान्मुक्तिदो भव ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> gopīcandana pāpaghna viṣṇudehasamudbhava ।
> cakrāṅkita namaste'stu dhāraṇānmuktido bhava ॥

## English meaning

O gopīcandana, destroyer of sin, born from the body of Viṣṇu, marked with the discus — salutations to you; by your being worn, become the giver of liberation.

## Action while reciting

Recited while applying the gopīcandana before marking the twelve nāmas on the body.
