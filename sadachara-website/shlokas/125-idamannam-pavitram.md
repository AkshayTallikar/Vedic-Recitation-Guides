# Idamannaṃ Pavitraṃ Syāt

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> इदमन्नं पवित्रं स्यात्पानीयं चातिपावनम् ।
> भुक्तपीतविशुद्ध्यर्थं हरेः पादोदकं पिबेत् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> idamannaṃ pavitraṃ syātpānīyaṃ cātipāvanam ।
> bhuktapītaviśuddhyarthaṃ hareḥ pādodakaṃ pibet ॥

## English meaning

May this food be pure and this drink most purifying; for the purification of all that is eaten and drunk, one should sip the foot-water (pādodaka) of Hari.

## Action while reciting

Recited during the meal while sipping the Lord's pādodaka (tīrtha), to sanctify the food and drink.
