# Yāni Kāni Ca Pāpāni (Pradakṣiṇa)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> यानि कानि च पापानि जन्मान्तरकृतानि च ।
> तानि तानि विनश्यन्ति प्रदक्षिणपदे पदे ॥
> तीर्थकोटिसहस्राणि व्रतकोटिशतानि च ।
> नारायणप्रणामस्य कलां नार्हन्ति षोडशीम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> yāni kāni ca pāpāni janmāntarakṛtāni ca ।
> tāni tāni vinaśyanti pradakṣiṇapade pade ॥
> tīrthakoṭisahasrāṇi vratakoṭiśatāni ca ।
> nārāyaṇapraṇāmasya kalāṃ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm ॥

## English meaning

Whatever sins have been committed in this and in other births — all of them perish with every single step of circumambulation. Thousands of crores of pilgrimages and hundreds of crores of vows are not worth even a sixteenth part of a single prostration to Nārāyaṇa.

## Action while reciting

Recited while performing pradakṣiṇa (circumambulation) and namaskāra at the close of worship.
