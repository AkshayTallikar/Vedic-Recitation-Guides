# Gadāmudrā — Gadādhara

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ब्रह्माण्डभुवनारम्भमूलस्तम्भो गदाधरः ।
> कौमोदकी करे यस्य तं नमामि गदाधरम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> brahmāṇḍabhuvanārambhamūlastambho gadādharaḥ ।
> kaumodakī kare yasya taṃ namāmi gadādharam ॥

## English meaning

Gadādhara, the foundational pillar at the origin of the cosmic egg and its worlds, in whose hand is the mace Kaumodakī — to that Gadādhara I bow.

## Action while reciting

Recited while wearing the **Gadāmudrā** (mace mark).
