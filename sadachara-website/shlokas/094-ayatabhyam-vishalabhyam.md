# Āyatābhyāṃ Viśālābhyāṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> आयताभ्यां विशालाभ्यां शीतलाभ्यां कृपानिधे ।
> करुणासरपूर्णाभ्यां लोचनाभ्यां विलोकय ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> āyatābhyāṃ viśālābhyāṃ śītalābhyāṃ kṛpānidhe ।
> karuṇāsarapūrṇābhyāṃ locanābhyāṃ vilokaya ॥

## English meaning

O ocean of compassion, look upon me with those eyes of Yours — long, wide, cool, and brimming with the nectar of mercy.

## Action while reciting

A prayer pleading with the Lord (the ocean of kindness) to cast His compassionate glance upon the worshipper.
