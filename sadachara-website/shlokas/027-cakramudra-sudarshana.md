# Cakramudrā — Sudarśana Mahājvāla

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> सुदर्शन महाज्वाल कोटिसूर्यसमप्रभ ।
> अज्ञानान्धस्य मे नित्यं विष्णोर्मार्गं प्रदर्शय ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> sudarśana mahājvāla koṭisūryasamaprabha ।
> ajñānāndhasya me nityaṃ viṣṇormārgaṃ pradarśaya ॥

## English meaning

O Sudarśana of great flames, radiant as a crore suns — to me, blinded by ignorance, ever show the path to Viṣṇu.

## Action while reciting

Recited while wearing the **Cakramudrā** (the discus mark), the first of the five mudrās.
