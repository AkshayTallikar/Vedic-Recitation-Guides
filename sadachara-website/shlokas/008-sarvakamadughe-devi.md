# Sarvakāmadughe Devi (Surabhi / Cow prayer)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> सर्वकामदुघे देवि सर्वतीर्थाभिषेचिनि ।
> पावनि सुरभे श्रेष्ठे देवि तुभ्यं नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> sarvakāmadughe devi sarvatīrthābhiṣecini ।
> pāvani surabhe śreṣṭhe devi tubhyaṃ namo'stu te ॥

## English meaning

O Goddess (cow) who yields all desires, in whom all sacred waters bathe; O purifying one, O excellent Surabhi, O Goddess, salutations to you.

## Action while reciting

Perform namaskāra to the cows. Then chant the *Gajendra Mokṣa* and the *Maṅgalāṣṭaka*, read the *pañcāṅga* (almanac). The bath follows.
