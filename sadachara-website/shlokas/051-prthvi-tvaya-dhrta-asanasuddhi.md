# Pṛthvi Tvayā Dhṛtā (Āsanaśuddhi)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> पृथ्वि त्वया धृता लोका देवि त्वं विष्णुना धृता ।
> त्वं च धारय मां देवि पवित्रं कुरु चासनम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> pṛthvi tvayā dhṛtā lokā devi tvaṃ viṣṇunā dhṛtā ।
> tvaṃ ca dhāraya māṃ devi pavitraṃ kuru cāsanam ॥

## English meaning

O Earth (Pṛthvī Devī), the worlds are upheld by you, and you yourself are upheld by Viṣṇu. O Goddess, uphold me too, and make my seat pure.

## Action while reciting

The Āsanaśuddhi — purification of the seat before the rite; a prayer to Bhū Devī. Viniyoga: *pṛthvītyasya merupṛṣṭha ṛṣiḥ; anuṣṭup chandaḥ; kūrmo devatā; āsanaśuddhyarthe jape viniyogaḥ.*
