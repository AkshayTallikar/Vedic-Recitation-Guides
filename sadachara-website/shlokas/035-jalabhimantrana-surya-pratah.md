# Jalābhimantraṇa — Sūryaśca (morning)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं सूर्यश्च मा मन्युश्च मन्युपतयश्च मन्युकृतेभ्यः ।
> पापेभ्यो रक्षन्ताम् ।
> यद्रात्र्या पापमकार्षम् ।
> मनसा वाचा हस्ताभ्याम् ।
> पद्भ्यामुदरेण शिश्ना ।
> रात्रिस्तदवलुम्पतु ।
> यत्किञ्च दुरितं मयि ।
> इदमहं माममृतयोनौ ।
> सूर्ये ज्योतिषि जुहोमि स्वाहा । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ sūryaśca mā manyuśca manyupatayaśca manyukṛtebhyaḥ ।
> pāpebhyo rakṣantām ।
> yadrātryā pāpamakārṣam ।
> manasā vācā hastābhyām ।
> padbhyāmudareṇa śiśnā ।
> rātristadavalumpatu ।
> yatkiñca duritaṃ mayi ।
> idamahaṃ māmamṛtayonau ।
> sūrye jyotiṣi juhomi svāhā । oṃ ।

## English meaning

May Sūrya, and Manyu (wrath), and the lords of wrath, protect me from the sins arising from anger. Whatever sin I have committed during the night — by mind, speech, hands, feet, belly, or organs — may the night wipe it away. Whatever evil there is in me, I offer it into Sūrya, the light, the immortal source — svāhā.

## Action while reciting

The morning Jalābhimantraṇa: hold a little water in the right palm and, on uttering *svāhā*, drink it (*iti jalaṃ prāśya*). The sin committed in the night is expiated. Viniyoga: *sūryaśceti asya mantrasya nārāyaṇa ṛṣiḥ; prakṛtiḥ chandaḥ; sūryo devatā; jalābhimantraṇe viniyogaḥ.*
