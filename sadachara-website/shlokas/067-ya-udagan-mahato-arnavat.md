# Ya Udagān Mahato'rṇavāt

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> य उदगान्महतोऽर्णवाद्विभ्राजमानः सलिलस्य मध्यात् ।
> अनानुदो वृषभो रोहिताक्षः सूर्यो विपश्चिन्मनसा पुनातु ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ya udagānmahato'rṇavādvibhrājamānaḥ salilasya madhyāt ।
> anānudo vṛṣabho rohitākṣaḥ sūryo vipaścinmanasā punātu ॥

## English meaning

He who rose shining from the great ocean, from the midst of the waters — the unyielding bull, the red-eyed one — may the wise Sūrya purify us with His mind.

## Action while reciting

A purificatory Sūrya mantra recited as part of the Sūryadarśana / Sūryopasthāna.
