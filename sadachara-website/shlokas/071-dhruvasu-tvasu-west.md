# Dhruvāsu Tvāsu (West — Varuṇa)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं ध्रुवासुत्वासु क्षितिषु क्षियन्तो व्यश्मत् पाशं वरुणो मुमोचत् ।
> अवो वन्वाना अदितेरुपस्थाद्यूयं पात स्वस्तिभिः सदा नः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ dhruvāsutvāsu kṣitiṣu kṣiyanto vyaśmat pāśaṃ varuṇo mumocat ।
> avo vanvānā aditerupasthādyūyaṃ pāta svastibhiḥ sadā naḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

Dwelling on these firm abodes, may Varuṇa loosen the noose from us. Winning favour from the lap of Aditi — may you all ever protect us with your blessings.

## Action while reciting

The third of the four direction-facing prayers — recited facing west. Viniyoga: *dhruvāsutveti vasiṣṭho varuṇas triṣṭup.*
