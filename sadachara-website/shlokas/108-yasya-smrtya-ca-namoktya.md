# Yasya Smṛtyā Ca Nāmoktyā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> यस्य स्मृत्या च नामोक्त्या तपःपूजाक्रियादिषु ।
> न्यूनं सम्पूर्णतां याति सद्यो वन्दे तमच्युतम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> yasya smṛtyā ca nāmoktyā tapaḥpūjākriyādiṣu ।
> nyūnaṃ sampūrṇatāṃ yāti sadyo vande tamacyutam ॥

## English meaning

By the mere remembrance of whom and the utterance of whose name, whatever is deficient in austerities, worship and rites at once attains completeness — that Acyuta I salute.

## Action while reciting

Recited at the conclusion of the pūjā to make good any deficiencies in the worship, invoking Acyuta whose remembrance perfects all rites.
