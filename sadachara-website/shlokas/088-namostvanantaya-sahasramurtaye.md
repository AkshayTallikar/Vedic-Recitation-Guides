# Namo'stvanantāya Sahasramūrtaye

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नमोऽस्त्वनन्ताय सहस्रमूर्तये सहस्रपादाक्षिशिरोरुबाहवे ।
> सहस्रनाम्ने पुरुषाय शाश्वते सहस्रकोटीयुगधारिणे नमः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> namo'stvanantāya sahasramūrtaye sahasrapādākṣiśirorubāhave ।
> sahasranāmne puruṣāya śāśvate sahasrakoṭīyugadhāriṇe namaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to the Infinite One of a thousand forms, of a thousand feet, eyes, heads and arms; to the Puruṣa of a thousand names, the eternal one who upholds a thousand crore ages — salutations.

## Action while reciting

A praṇāma to the cosmic, infinite form of Viṣṇu (Ananta), offered during worship.
