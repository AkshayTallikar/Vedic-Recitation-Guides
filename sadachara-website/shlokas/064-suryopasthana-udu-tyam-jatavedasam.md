# Sūryopasthāna — Udu Tyaṃ Jātavedasaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं उदु त्यं जातवेदसं देवं वहन्ति केतवः ।
> दृशे विश्वाय सूर्यम् । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ udu tyaṃ jātavedasaṃ devaṃ vahanti ketavaḥ ।
> dṛśe viśvāya sūryam । oṃ ।

## English meaning

The rays bear aloft that divine Sūrya, the knower of all beings (Jātavedas), so that all may behold him.

## Action while reciting

A Sūryopasthāna mantra. Touch the right shoulder with the right hand and the left shoulder with the left hand while reciting.
