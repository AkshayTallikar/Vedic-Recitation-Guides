# Ehi Tvaṃ Devi Gāyatri (Gāyatrī invocation)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> एहि त्वं देवि शीघ्रं वै गायत्रि ब्रह्मरूपिणि ।
> जपानुष्ठानसिद्ध्यर्थं प्रविश्य हृदयं मम ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ehi tvaṃ devi śīghraṃ vai gāyatri brahmarūpiṇi ।
> japānuṣṭhānasiddhyarthaṃ praviśya hṛdayaṃ mama ॥

## English meaning

Come quickly, O Goddess Gāyatrī, you whose form is Brahman; for the success of my japa and observance, enter into my heart.

## Action while reciting

Recited to draw the Gāyatrī into the heart (gāyatryākarṣaṇa), followed by "*gāyatrī ca mahādevi praviśya hṛdayaṃ mama; asāvādityo brahma; omāpojyotiraso'mṛtaṃ brahma bhūrbhuvaḥsvarom*." Then circumambulate oneself with water and perform ācamana twice.
