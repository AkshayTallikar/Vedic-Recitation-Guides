# Tulasī Dhyāna — Dhyāyāmi Tulasīṃ Devīṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ध्यायामि तुलसीं देवीं श्यामां कमललोचनाम् ।
> प्रसन्नां पद्मकह्लारवराभयचतुर्भुजाम् ॥
> किरीटहारकेयूरकुण्डलादिविभूषिताम् ।
> धवलांशुकसंयुक्तां पद्मासननिषेदुषीम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> dhyāyāmi tulasīṃ devīṃ śyāmāṃ kamalalocanām ।
> prasannāṃ padmakahlāravarābhayacaturbhujām ॥
> kirīṭahārakeyūrakuṇḍalādivibhūṣitām ।
> dhavalāṃśukasaṃyuktāṃ padmāsananiṣeduṣīm ॥

## English meaning

I meditate on the goddess Tulasī — dark-hued, lotus-eyed, serene, four-armed, holding lotus and white-lily and showing the boon and abhaya gestures; adorned with crown, garland, armlets, earrings and other ornaments; clad in white garments, seated upon a lotus.

## Action while reciting

The dhyāna of Tulasī, recited in the **Tulasī Pūjā** (celebrated from Kārtika śukla pādya to dvādaśī, after the daily evening pūjā). Perform śaṅkha-pūjā, ṣṭha-pūjā, pūjā to the Lord with the Puruṣa Sūkta, then chant "gaṅge ca yamune caiva…" and worship Tulasī.
