# Prasīda Tulasī Devi (Tulasī Prārthanā)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> प्रसीद तुलसी देवि प्रसीद हरिवल्लभे ।
> क्षीरोदमथनोद्भूते तुलसि त्वां नमाम्यहम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> prasīda tulasī devi prasīda harivallabhe ।
> kṣīrodamathanodbhūte tulasi tvāṃ namāmyaham ॥

## English meaning

Be gracious, O goddess Tulasī; be gracious, O beloved of Hari. O Tulasī, arisen from the churning of the ocean of milk, I bow to you.

## Action while reciting

The Tulasī-prārthanā, recited while gathering tulasī leaves for the worship.
