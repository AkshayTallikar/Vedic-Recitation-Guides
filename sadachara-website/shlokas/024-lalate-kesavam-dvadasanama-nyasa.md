# Lalāṭe Keśavaṃ — Twelve-Nāma Placement

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ललाटे केशवं विद्यात् नारायणमथोदरे ।
> माधवं हृदये विद्यात् गोविन्दं कण्टकूबरे ॥
> उदरे दक्षिणे पार्श्वे विष्णुं विद्यात् विचक्षणः ।
> तत्पार्श्वे बाहुमूले तु धारयेत् मधुसूदनम् ॥
> त्रिविक्रमं कण्ठदेशे वामपार्श्वे तु वामनम् ।
> श्रीधरं वामबाहौ तु हृषीकेशं गले लिखेत् ॥
> पृष्ठे तु पद्मनाभं तु ककुद्दामोदरं न्यसेत् ।
> वासुदेवेति मूर्धनि ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> lalāṭe keśavaṃ vidyāt nārāyaṇamathodare ।
> mādhavaṃ hṛdaye vidyāt govindaṃ kaṇṭakūbare ॥
> udare dakṣiṇe pārśve viṣṇuṃ vidyāt vicakṣaṇaḥ ।
> tatpārśve bāhumūle tu dhārayet madhusūdanam ॥
> trivikramaṃ kaṇṭhadeśe vāmapārśve tu vāmanam ।
> śrīdharaṃ vāmabāhau tu hṛṣīkeśaṃ gale likhet ॥
> pṛṣṭhe tu padmanābhaṃ tu kakuddāmodaraṃ nyaset ।
> vāsudeveti mūrdhani ॥

## English meaning

One should know Keśava on the forehead, Nārāyaṇa on the belly, Mādhava on the heart, Govinda at the throat-hollow; the wise should place Viṣṇu on the right side of the belly, Madhusūdana on the root of the (right) arm; Trivikrama at the throat region, Vāmana on the left side, Śrīdhara on the left arm, Hṛṣīkeśa marked on the neck; Padmanābha on the back, Dāmodara on the nape, and Vāsudeva on the head.

## Action while reciting

The placement (*nyāsa*) of the twelve ūrdhvapuṇḍra marks with their presiding nāmas on the corresponding parts of the body.
