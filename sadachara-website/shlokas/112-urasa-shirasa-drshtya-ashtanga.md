# Urasā Śirasā Dṛṣṭyā (Aṣṭāṅga Praṇāma)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> उरसा शिरसा दृष्ट्या मनसा वचसा तथा ।
> पद्भ्यां कराभ्यां जानुभ्यां प्रणामोऽष्टाङ्ग ईरितः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> urasā śirasā dṛṣṭyā manasā vacasā tathā ।
> padbhyāṃ karābhyāṃ jānubhyāṃ praṇāmo'ṣṭāṅga īritaḥ ॥

## English meaning

With the chest, the head, the eyes, the mind and speech, and with the feet, the hands and the knees — such is declared to be the eight-limbed (aṣṭāṅga) prostration.

## Action while reciting

Defines and accompanies the sāṣṭāṅga praṇāma — the full eight-limbed prostration offered to the Lord.
