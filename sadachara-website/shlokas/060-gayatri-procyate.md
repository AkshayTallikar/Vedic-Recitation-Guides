# Gāyatrī Procyate (why it is called Gāyatrī)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> प्रतिग्रहादन्नदोषात्पातकादुपपातकात् ।
> गायत्री प्रोच्यते तस्मात् गायन्तं त्रायते यतः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> pratigrahādannadoṣātpātakādupapātakāt ।
> gāyatrī procyate tasmāt gāyantaṃ trāyate yataḥ ॥

## English meaning

Because it protects the one who sings it (*gāyantaṃ trāyate*) from the faults of accepting gifts and of food, and from major and minor sins — therefore it is called "Gāyatrī."

## Action while reciting

A verse explaining the power and the very name of the Gāyatrī; it is this mantra that is given on the upanayana day as the brahmopadeśa.
