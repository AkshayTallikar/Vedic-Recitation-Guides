# Karaśuddhi (Hand purification)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं यं । ओं रं । ओं वं । (एवं चतुर्वारं) अस्त्राय फट् ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ yaṃ । oṃ raṃ । oṃ vaṃ । (evaṃ caturvāraṃ) astrāya phaṭ ।

## English meaning

Oṃ yaṃ, Oṃ raṃ, Oṃ vaṃ (the seed-syllables of air, fire and water, repeated four times); astrāya phaṭ (to the weapon).

## Action while reciting

The Karaśuddhi — purification of the hands. Recite the bīja-mantras (repeating four times), then "astrāya phaṭ" and clap the palms. Touching the wrist, mid-palm and fingertips of both hands, front and back, is the essential purificatory process.
