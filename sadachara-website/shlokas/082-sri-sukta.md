# Śrī Sūkta (opening verses)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> हिरण्यवर्णां हरिणीं सुवर्णरजतस्रजाम् ।
> चन्द्रां हिरण्मयीं लक्ष्मीं जातवेदो म आवह ॥
> तां म आवह जातवेदो लक्ष्मीमनपगामिनीम् ।
> यस्यां हिरण्यं विन्देयं गामश्वं पुरुषानहम् ॥
> अश्वपूर्वां रथमध्यां हस्तिनादप्रमोदिनीम् ।
> श्रियं देवीमुपह्वये श्रीर्मा देवी जुषताम् ॥
> कां सोस्मितां हिरण्यप्राकारामार्द्रां ज्वलन्तीं तृप्तां तर्पयन्तीम् ।
> पद्मे स्थितां पद्मवर्णां तामिहोपह्वये श्रियम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> hiraṇyavarṇāṃ hariṇīṃ suvarṇarajatasrajām ।
> candrāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ lakṣmīṃ jātavedo ma āvaha ॥
> tāṃ ma āvaha jātavedo lakṣmīmanapagāminīm ।
> yasyāṃ hiraṇyaṃ vindeyaṃ gāmaśvaṃ puruṣānaham ॥
> aśvapūrvāṃ rathamadhyāṃ hastinādapramodinīm ।
> śriyaṃ devīmupahvaye śrīrmā devī juṣatām ॥
> kāṃ sosmitāṃ hiraṇyaprākārāmārdrāṃ jvalantīṃ tṛptāṃ tarpayantīm ।
> padme sthitāṃ padmavarṇāṃ tāmihopahvaye śriyam ॥

## English meaning

O Jātavedas (Agni), summon for me Lakṣmī — golden-hued, fawn-like, garlanded with gold and silver, moon-bright, made of gold. Summon for me that Lakṣmī who never departs, by whom I may obtain gold, cattle, horses and people. Heralded by horses, with chariots in her midst, delighting in the trumpeting of elephants — I invoke the goddess Śrī; may the goddess Śrī favour me. The gently-smiling one, ringed with gold, moist (compassionate), blazing, contented and contenting, seated on a lotus and lotus-hued — that Śrī I invoke here.

## Action while reciting

The opening verses of the **Śrī Sūkta** (a fifteen-verse hymn to Lakṣmī, ṛṣis Ānanda-Kardama-Śrīda-Ciklīta, the children of Indirā; devatā Śrī), recited in Lakṣmī worship. Continues with "*candrāṃ prabhāsāṃ yaśasā jvalantīm…*", "*ādityavarṇe tapaso'dhijāto…*", etc.
