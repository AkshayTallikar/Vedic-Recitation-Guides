# Nārāyaṇamudrā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नारायण नमस्तेऽस्तु नाममुद्राङ्कितं नरम् ।
> दृष्ट्वैव लभते मुक्तिं चण्डालो ब्रह्मघातकः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> nārāyaṇa namaste'stu nāmamudrāṅkitaṃ naram ।
> dṛṣṭvaiva labhate muktiṃ caṇḍālo brahmaghātakaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to you, O Nārāyaṇa. Merely on seeing a person marked with the nāma-mudrās, even a caṇḍāla or a slayer of a brāhmaṇa attains liberation.

## Action while reciting

Recited while wearing the **Nārāyaṇamudrā**, which should be applied wherever a nāma has been applied. This completes the five mudrās.
