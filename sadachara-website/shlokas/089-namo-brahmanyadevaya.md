# Namo Brahmaṇyadevāya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नमो ब्रह्मण्यदेवाय गोब्राह्मणहिताय च ।
> जगद्धिताय कृष्णाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> namo brahmaṇyadevāya gobrāhmaṇahitāya ca ।
> jagaddhitāya kṛṣṇāya govindāya namo namaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to the God devoted to the brahmins, to the benefactor of cows and brahmins, to the well-wisher of the whole world — to Kṛṣṇa, to Govinda, salutations again and again.

## Action while reciting

The well-known Kṛṣṇa praṇāma mantra, recited in worship as a salutation to Kṛṣṇa-Govinda.
