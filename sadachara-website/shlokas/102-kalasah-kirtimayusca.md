# Kalaśaḥ Kīrtimāyuśca

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> कलशः कीर्तिमायुश्च प्रज्ञां मेधां श्रियं भगम् ।
> योग्यतां पापहानिं च पुण्यवृद्धिं च साधयेत् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> kalaśaḥ kīrtimāyuśca prajñāṃ medhāṃ śriyaṃ bhagam ।
> yogyatāṃ pāpahāniṃ ca puṇyavṛddhiṃ ca sādhayet ॥

## English meaning

May the (worshipped) kalaśa bestow fame and long life, intelligence, retentive wisdom, prosperity and fortune; worthiness, the removal of sins, and the increase of merit.

## Action while reciting

The concluding phala-verse of the kalaśa-pūjā, recited to invoke the benefits the consecrated water-pot confers, before its water is used in the worship.
