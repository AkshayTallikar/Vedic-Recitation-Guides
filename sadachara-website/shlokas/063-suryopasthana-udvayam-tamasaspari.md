# Sūryopasthāna — Ud Vayaṃ Tamasaspari

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं उद्वयं तमसस्परि ज्योतिष्पश्यन्त उत्तरम् ।
> देवं देवत्रा सूर्यमगन्म ज्योतिरुत्तमम् । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ udvayaṃ tamasaspari jyotiṣpaśyanta uttaram ।
> devaṃ devatrā sūryamaganma jyotiruttamam । oṃ ।

## English meaning

Beholding the higher light beyond the darkness, we have reached the divine Sūrya among the gods — the highest light.

## Action while reciting

The Sūryopasthāna (worship of the Sun), recited at noon (and as part of the sandhyā). Stand facing east, join the palms, and pray.
