# Gāyatrī Dhyāna — Dhyeyaḥ Sadā Savitṛmaṇḍala

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ध्येयः सदा सवितृमण्डलमध्यवर्ती
> नारायणः सरसिजासनसन्निविष्टः ।
> केयूरवान् मकरकुण्डलवान् किरीटी
> हारी हिरण्मयवपुर्धृतशङ्खचक्रः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> dhyeyaḥ sadā savitṛmaṇḍalamadhyavartī
> nārāyaṇaḥ sarasijāsanasanniviṣṭaḥ ।
> keyūravān makarakuṇḍalavān kirīṭī
> hārī hiraṇmayavapurdhṛtaśaṅkhacakraḥ ॥

## English meaning

Ever to be meditated upon is Nārāyaṇa, dwelling in the centre of the sun's orb, seated on a lotus — wearing armlets, makara earrings, a crown and a garland, His body golden, bearing the conch and the discus.

## Action while reciting

The dhyāna of the Gāyatrī, meditating on Nārāyaṇa within the solar orb before the japa. (Preceded by: *oṃ procadādityavarṇaśca sūryamaṇḍalamadhyagaḥ; cakraśaṅkhadharo'ṅkasthadordvayo dhyeya eva ca.*)
