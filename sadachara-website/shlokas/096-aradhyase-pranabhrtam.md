# Ārādhyase Prāṇabhṛtāṃ Praṇetrā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> आराध्यसे प्राणभृतां प्रणेत्रा प्राणाधिनाथेन समीरणेन ।
> नारायण ज्ञानसुखैकपूर्ण स्वामिन् मम श्रीरमण प्रसीद ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ārādhyase prāṇabhṛtāṃ praṇetrā prāṇādhināthena samīraṇena ।
> nārāyaṇa jñānasukhaikapūrṇa svāmin mama śrīramaṇa prasīda ॥

## English meaning

You are worshipped by Vāyu (Samīraṇa), the leader and lord of all living beings' vital breath. O Nārāyaṇa, full of the one bliss that is knowledge, my Lord, beloved of Śrī — be gracious to me.

## Action while reciting

Recited just before the pūjā: an acknowledgement that it is truly Mukhyaprāṇa (Vāyu, the jīvottama) within who performs the worship of Lakṣmīnārāyaṇa, with a prayer for the Lord's grace.
