# Prātaḥkālādikaṃ Karma (Saṅkalpa)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> प्रातःकालादिकं कर्म नित्यनैमित्तिकं तथा ।
> केशवाराधनार्थाय करिष्येऽहं तवाज्ञया ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> prātaḥkālādikaṃ karma nityanaimittikaṃ tathā ।
> keśavārādhanārthāya kariṣye'haṃ tavājñayā ॥

## English meaning

The morning and other duties, both obligatory (*nitya*) and occasional (*naimittika*), I shall perform for the worship of Keśava, by Your command — *nityakarma* such as japa and pūjā, *naimittika karma* such as pitṛ-śrāddha.

## Action while reciting

Recited as the saṅkalpa (resolve) before beginning the day's rites.
