# Śrīmadveṅkaṭanāthāya Śrīnivāsāya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> कल्याणाद्भुतगात्राय कामितार्थप्रदायिने ।
> श्रीमद्वेङ्कटनाथाय श्रीनिवासाय ते नमः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> kalyāṇādbhutagātrāya kāmitārthapradāyine ।
> śrīmadveṅkaṭanāthāya śrīnivāsāya te namaḥ ॥

## English meaning

To the one of auspicious and wondrous form, the granter of all desired ends — to the glorious Lord of Veṅkaṭa, to Śrīnivāsa, salutations to You.

## Action while reciting

A salutation to Lord Veṅkaṭeśa (Śrīnivāsa), recited within the namaskāra-stuti at the close of worship.
