# Gurunamaskāra

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> श्री गुरुभ्यो नमः । पूर्वगुरुभ्यो नमः । आदिगुरुभ्यो नमः ।
> मूलगुरुभ्यो नमः । मूलदेवताभ्यो नमः । वासुदेवाय नमः ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> śrī gurubhyo namaḥ । pūrvagurubhyo namaḥ । ādigurubhyo namaḥ ।
> mūlagurubhyo namaḥ । mūladevatābhyo namaḥ । vāsudevāya namaḥ ।

## English meaning

Salutations to the revered gurus; to the earlier gurus; to the first guru; to the root guru; to the root deities; and to Vāsudeva.

## Action while reciting

The Gurunamaskāra — salutation to all the preceptors, essential at the beginning of any karma.
