# Namo Vastāttvikā Devāḥ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नमो वस्तात्त्विका देवाः विष्णुभक्तिपरायणाः ।
> धर्ममार्गे प्रेरयन्तु भवन्तः सर्व एव हि ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> namo vastāttvikā devāḥ viṣṇubhaktiparāyaṇāḥ ।
> dharmamārge prerayantu bhavantaḥ sarva eva hi ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to the *tattva-devatās*, the true deities devoted to Lord Viṣṇu. May you all inspire me to progress on the path of dharma.

## Action while reciting

The book instructs chanting this 16 times.
