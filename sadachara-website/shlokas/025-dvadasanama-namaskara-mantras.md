# Dvādaśanāma Namaskāra Mantras

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ॐ वासुदेवाय नमः ।
> ॐ केशवाय नमः ।
> ॐ नारायणाय नमः ।
> ॐ माधवाय नमः ।
> ॐ गोविन्दाय नमः ।
> ॐ विष्णवे नमः ।
> ॐ मधुसूदनाय नमः ।
> ॐ त्रिविक्रमाय नमः ।
> ॐ वामनाय नमः ।
> ॐ श्रीधराय नमः ।
> ॐ हृषीकेशाय नमः ।
> ॐ पद्मनाभाय नमः ।
> ॐ दामोदराय नमः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ vāsudevāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ keśavāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ nārāyaṇāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ mādhavāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ govindāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ viṣṇave namaḥ ।
> oṃ madhusūdanāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ trivikramāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ vāmanāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ śrīdharāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ hṛṣīkeśāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ padmanābhāya namaḥ ।
> oṃ dāmodarāya namaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to the twelve forms of the Lord — Vāsudeva, Keśava, Nārāyaṇa, Mādhava, Govinda, Viṣṇu, Madhusūdana, Trivikrama, Vāmana, Śrīdhara, Hṛṣīkeśa, Padmanābha and Dāmodara.

## Action while reciting

Apply the nāma with the forefinger on each body-part as each mantra is chanted: Vāsudeva on the crown; Keśava on the forehead (from the edge of the nose, with a gap in the middle); Nārāyaṇa on the middle of the stomach; Mādhava on the chest; Govinda at the middle of the throat; Viṣṇu on the right of the stomach; Madhusūdana on the right forearm; Trivikrama on the right of the neck; Vāmana on the left of the stomach; Śrīdhara on the left forearm; Hṛṣīkeśa on the left of the neck; Padmanābha on the middle of the back; Dāmodara on the back of the neck.
