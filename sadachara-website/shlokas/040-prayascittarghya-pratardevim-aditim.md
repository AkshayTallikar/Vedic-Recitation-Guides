# Prāyaścittārghya — Prātardevīmaditiṃ (noon)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं प्रातर्देवीमदितिं जोहवीमि मध्यं दिन उदिता सूर्यस्य ।
> राये मित्रावरुणा सर्वतातेळे तोकाय तनयाय शं योः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ prātardevīmaditiṃ johavīmi madhyaṃ dina uditā sūryasya ।
> rāye mitrāvaruṇā sarvatāteḻe tokāya tanayāya śaṃ yoḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

I invoke the divine Aditi at dawn, at midday, and at the rising of the Sun. O Mitra and Varuṇa, I implore you in full measure for wealth, and for the well-being and peace of our children and progeny.

## Action while reciting

The noon (mādhyāhna) Prāyaścittārghya; offer one arghya with this mantra. Viniyoga: *prātardevīmityasya mantrasya ātreya urucakri ṛṣiḥ; triṣṭup chandaḥ; mitrāvaruṇau devate; mādhyāhnakālātīta-doṣaprāyaścittārghyapradāne viniyogaḥ.*
