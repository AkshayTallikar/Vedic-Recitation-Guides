# Gāyatrī Visarjana — Uttiṣṭha Devi

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> उत्तिष्ठ देवि गन्तव्यं पुनरागमनाय च ।
> गायत्री च महादेवि प्रविश्य हृदयं मम ॥
> असावादित्यो ब्रह्म ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> uttiṣṭha devi gantavyaṃ punarāgamanāya ca ।
> gāyatrī ca mahādevi praviśya hṛdayaṃ mama ॥
> asāvādityo brahma ।

## English meaning

Arise, O Goddess, you may now depart, to come again (at the next sandhyā). O great Goddess Gāyatrī, having entered my heart (may you remain there). That sun yonder is Brahman.

## Action while reciting

Recited immediately after the Gāyatryākarṣaṇa mantra (*prati cakṣva*, shloka 042), bidding the Goddess Gāyatrī leave the external oblation-water and abide in the heart. Followed by repeating the *oṃ āpo jyotī rasaḥ...bhūrbhuvaḥsvarom* formula (shloka 033), after which one sprinkles/circumambulates oneself with the water (*iti jalena ātmānaṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ kuryāt*) and does ācamana twice.
