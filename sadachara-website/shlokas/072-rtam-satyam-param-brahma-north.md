# Ṛtaṃ Satyaṃ Paraṃ Brahma (North)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं ऋतं सत्यं परं ब्रह्म पुरुषं कृष्णपिङ्गलम् ।
> ऊर्ध्वरेतं विरूपाक्षं विश्वरूपाय वै नमो नमः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ ṛtaṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma puruṣaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgalam ।
> ūrdhvaretaṃ virūpākṣaṃ viśvarūpāya vai namo namaḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

To the cosmic order, the truth, the supreme Brahman, the Puruṣa dark-and-tawny, the one of uplifted vigour, the odd-eyed one — to the one of universal form, salutations again and again.

## Action while reciting

The fourth of the four direction-facing prayers — recited facing north.
