# Agastirmādhavaścaiva (Sukhaśāyinaḥ)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> अगस्तिर्माधवश्चैव मुचुकुन्दो महामुनिः ।
> कपिलो मुनिरास्तीकः पञ्चैते सुखशायिनः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> agastirmādhavaścaiva mucukundo mahāmuniḥ ।
> kapilo munirāstīkaḥ pañcaite sukhaśāyinaḥ ॥

## English meaning

Agastya, and Mādhava, Mucukunda the great sage, Kapila, and the sage Āstīka — these five are the ones who sleep in peace.

## Action while reciting

Recited at bedtime (śayana), remembering the five who 'sleep happily', as a prayer for undisturbed, peaceful sleep.
