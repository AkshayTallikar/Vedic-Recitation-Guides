# Apasarpantu Te Bhūtā (Bhūtoccāṭana)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> अपसर्पन्तु ते भूता ये भूता भुवि संस्थिताः ।
> ये भूता विघ्नकर्तारः ते नश्यन्तु शिवाज्ञया ॥
> भूतप्रेतपिशाचा ये ये चान्ये भुवि भारकाः ।
> तेषामप्यविरोधेन जपकर्म समारभे ॥
> निरस्तः परावसुः । इदमहमर्वावसोः सदने सीदामि ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> apasarpantu te bhūtā ye bhūtā bhuvi saṃsthitāḥ ।
> ye bhūtā vighnakartāraḥ te naśyantu śivājñayā ॥
> bhūtapretapiśācā ye ye cānye bhuvi bhārakāḥ ।
> teṣāmapyavirodhena japakarma samārabhe ॥
> nirastaḥ parāvasuḥ । idamahamarvāvasoḥ sadane sīdāmi ।

## English meaning

Let those spirits depart that dwell upon the earth; and those spirits that cause obstacles, let them perish by the command of the auspicious Lord. Whatever bhūtas, pretas, piśācas and other burdensome beings are on earth — even without conflict with them, I begin the work of japa. The far-dwelling (Parāvasu) is driven off; I take my seat here in the abode of the near-dwelling (Arvāvasu).

## Action while reciting

The Bhūtoccāṭana — driving away obstructing spirits before beginning japa. Viniyoga: *apasarpantvityasya mantrasya vāmadeva ṛṣiḥ; anuṣṭup chandaḥ; satyo devatā; samastabhūtoccāṭane viniyogaḥ.* (A variant adds: *ye cātra nivasantyeva devatāḥ bhuvi santatam, teṣāmapyavirodhena japakarma samārabhe.*)
