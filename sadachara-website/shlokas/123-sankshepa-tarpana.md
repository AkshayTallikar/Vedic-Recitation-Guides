# Saṅkṣepa Tarpaṇa

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> आ ब्रह्मस्तम्बपर्यन्तं यत्किञ्चित् सचराचरम् ।
> मया दत्तेन तोयेन तृप्तिमायान्तु पुष्कलम् ॥
> ये मे चास्मत्कुले जाता अपुत्रा गोत्रिणो मृताः ।
> ते गृह्णन्तु मया दत्तं वस्त्रनिष्पीडनोदकम् ॥
> येषां पिता न च भ्राता न पुत्रो नान्यगोत्रिणः ।
> ते सर्वे तृप्तिमायान्तु मयोत्सृष्टैः कुशैस्तथा ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ā brahmastambaparyantaṃ yatkiñcit sacarācaram ।
> mayā dattena toyena tṛptimāyāntu puṣkalam ॥
> ye me cāsmatkule jātā aputrā gotriṇo mṛtāḥ ।
> te gṛhṇantu mayā dattaṃ vastraniṣpīḍanodakam ॥
> yeṣāṃ pitā na ca bhrātā na putro nānyagotriṇaḥ ।
> te sarve tṛptimāyāntu mayotsṛṣṭaiḥ kuśaistathā ॥

## English meaning

May whatever exists, moving and unmoving, from Brahmā down to a blade of grass, attain full satisfaction by the water I offer. May those born in my family who have died without sons, of my lineage, receive the water wrung from my cloth that I offer. And those who have no father, no brother, no son, nor any other kinsman — may they all be satisfied by the kuśa-grass (and water) cast out by me.

## Action while reciting

The saṅkṣepa (abbreviated) tarpaṇa, recited by those unable to perform the full ancestor-offering — offering water for the satisfaction of all beings and of departed relatives who have no one to offer for them.
