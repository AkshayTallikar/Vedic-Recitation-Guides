# Devatārghya Tarpaṇa — Dvādaśāditya (madhyāhna)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं धातारं तर्पयामि । ओं अर्यमणं तर्पयामि । ओं मित्रं तर्पयामि ।
> ओं वरुणं तर्पयामि । ओं अंशुं तर्पयामि । ओं भगं तर्पयामि ।
> ओं इन्द्रं तर्पयामि । ओं विवस्वन्तं तर्पयामि । ओं पूषाणं तर्पयामि ।
> ओं त्वष्टारं तर्पयामि । ओं पर्जन्यं तर्पयामि । ओं विष्णुं तर्पयामि ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ dhātāraṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ aryamaṇaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ mitraṃ tarpayāmi ।
> oṃ varuṇaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ aṃśuṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ bhagaṃ tarpayāmi ।
> oṃ indraṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ vivasvantaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ pūṣāṇaṃ tarpayāmi ।
> oṃ tvaṣṭāraṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ parjanyaṃ tarpayāmi । oṃ viṣṇuṃ tarpayāmi ।

## English meaning

I offer tarpaṇa to Dhātṛ, Aryaman, Mitra, Varuṇa, Aṃśa, Bhaga, Indra, Vivasvān, Pūṣan, Tvaṣṭṛ, Parjanya and Viṣṇu — the twelve Ādityas.

## Action while reciting

The noon (madhyāhna) devatārghya-tarpaṇa: the twelve Ādityas are worshipped by name, pouring water with the ring finger, middle finger and forefinger — parallel to the bright-fortnight Keśavādi list (shloka 044) and dark-fortnight Saṅkarṣaṇādi list (shloka 045) offered at the morning sandhyā. Followed by ācamana twice. (On ekādaśī day, this noon tarpaṇa is done with the Keśavādi names instead.)
