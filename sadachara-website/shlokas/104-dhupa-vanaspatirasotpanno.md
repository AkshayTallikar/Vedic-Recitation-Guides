# Dhūpa — Vanaspatirasotpanno

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> वनस्पतिरसोत्पन्नो गन्धाढ्यो धूप उत्तमः ।
> आघ्रेयः सर्वदेवानां धूपोऽयं प्रतिगृह्यताम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> vanaspatirasotpanno gandhāḍhyo dhūpa uttamaḥ ।
> āghreyaḥ sarvadevānāṃ dhūpo'yaṃ pratigṛhyatām ॥

## English meaning

Arisen from the sap of plants, rich in fragrance, the finest incense — fit to be smelled by all the gods: may this incense be accepted.

## Action while reciting

Recited while offering dhūpa (incense) to the Lord during the pūjā upacāras.
