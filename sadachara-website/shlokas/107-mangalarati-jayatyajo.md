# Maṅgalārati — Jayatyajo'khaṇḍaguṇa

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं जयत्यजोऽखण्डगुणोरुमण्डलः सदोदितो ज्ञानमरीचिमाली ।
> स्वभक्तहार्दोच्चतमोनिहन्ता व्यासावतारो हरिरात्मभास्करः ॥१॥
> जयत्यजोऽक्षीणसुखात्मबिम्बः स्वैश्वर्यकान्तिप्रततः सदोदितः ।
> स्वभक्तसन्तापदुरिष्टहन्ता रामावतारो हरिरिशचन्द्रमाः ॥२॥
> जयत्यसङ्ख्योरुबलाम्बुपूरो गुणोच्चरत्नाकर आत्मवैभवः ।
> सदा सदात्मज्ञानदीभिराप्यः कृष्णावतारो हरिरेकसागरः ॥३॥
> जयति हरिरचिन्त्यः सर्वदेवैकवन्द्यः परम गुरुरभीष्टावाप्तिदः सज्जनानाम् ।
> निखिलगुणगणार्णो नित्यनिर्मुक्तदोषः सरसिजनयनोऽसौ श्रीपतिर्मानदो नः ॥४॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ jayatyajo'khaṇḍaguṇorumaṇḍalaḥ sadodito jñānamarīcimālī ।
> svabhaktahārdoccatamonihantā vyāsāvatāro harirātmabhāskaraḥ ॥1॥
> jayatyajo'kṣīṇasukhātmabimbaḥ svaiśvaryakāntipratataḥ sadoditaḥ ।
> svabhaktasantāpaduriṣṭahantā rāmāvatāro haririśacandramāḥ ॥2॥
> jayatyasaṅkhyorubalāmbupūro guṇoccaratnākara ātmavaibhavaḥ ।
> sadā sadātmajñānadībhirāpyaḥ kṛṣṇāvatāro harirekasāgaraḥ ॥3॥
> jayati hariracintyaḥ sarvadevaikavandyaḥ parama gururabhīṣṭāvāptidaḥ sajjanānām ।
> nikhilaguṇagaṇārṇo nityanirmuktadoṣaḥ sarasijanayano'sau śrīpatirmānado naḥ ॥4॥

## English meaning

Victory to Hari as **Vyāsa** — the unborn one whose vast orb of qualities is undivided, ever-arisen, garlanded with the rays of knowledge, who destroys the dense darkness in the hearts of His devotees, the self-luminous sun. Victory to Hari as **Rāma** — unborn, His very form pure undecaying bliss, ever radiant with the splendour of His own sovereignty, who slays the suffering and misfortune of His devotees, the Lord who is (like) the moon. Victory to Hari as **Kṛṣṇa** — flooded with the waters of immeasurable, great power, the jewel-ocean of qualities, of self-existent majesty, reached ever through the radiance of constant Self-knowledge, the one shoreless ocean. Victory to **Hari** — the inconceivable, sole adorable of all the gods, the supreme guru who grants the good their desires, the ocean of all hosts of qualities, eternally free of every fault, the lotus-eyed Lord of Śrī who bestows honour upon us.

## Action while reciting

The maṅgalārati stuti, chanted while performing the ārati (lamp-waving) at the close of the pūjā — saluting Hari's victorious manifestations as Vyāsa, Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, and as Śrīpati Himself. (Verses 1–3 are in vaṃśastha metre, verse 4 in mālinī.)
