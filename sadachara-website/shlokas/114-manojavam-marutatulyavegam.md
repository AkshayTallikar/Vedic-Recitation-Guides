# Manojavaṃ Mārutatulyavegaṃ (Hanumān)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम् ।
> वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> manojavaṃ mārutatulyavegaṃ jitendriyaṃ buddhimatāṃ variṣṭham ।
> vātātmajaṃ vānarayūthamukhyaṃ śrīrāmadūtaṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye ॥

## English meaning

Swift as thought, equal in speed to the wind, master of the senses, foremost among the wise — the son of Vāyu, chief of the monkey host, the messenger of Śrī Rāma: in him I take refuge.

## Action while reciting

The well-known śaraṇāgati to Hanumān (Mukhyaprāṇa), recited as a salutation; here concluding the namaskāra-stuti at the close of worship.
