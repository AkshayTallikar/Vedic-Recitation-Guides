# Sarvavighna-praśamanaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> सर्वविघ्नप्रशमनं सर्वसिद्धिकरं परम् ।
> सर्वजीवप्रणेतारं वन्दे विजयदं हरिम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> sarvavighnapraśamanaṃ sarvasiddhikaraṃ param ।
> sarvajīvapraṇetāraṃ vande vijayadaṃ harim ॥

## English meaning

I salute Hari, the giver of victory — queller of all obstacles, granter of all attainments, supreme, and guide of all living beings.

## Action while reciting

A salutation verse of the Hariguru-dhyāna.
