# Vede Rāmāyaṇe Caiva (Viṣṇuḥ Sarvatra Gīyate)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> वेदे रामायणे चैव पुराणे भारते तथा ।
> आदावन्ते च मध्ये च विष्णुः सर्वत्र गीयते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> vede rāmāyaṇe caiva purāṇe bhārate tathā ।
> ādāvante ca madhye ca viṣṇuḥ sarvatra gīyate ॥

## English meaning

In the Veda, in the Rāmāyaṇa, in the Purāṇas and the Mahābhārata — at the beginning, the middle, and the end — Viṣṇu alone is sung everywhere.

## Action while reciting

A dhyāna verse affirming that all scriptures everywhere praise Viṣṇu.
