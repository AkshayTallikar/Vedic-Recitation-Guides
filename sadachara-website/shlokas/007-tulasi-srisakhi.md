# Tulasi Śrīsakhi

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> तुलसि श्रीसखि शुभे पापहारिणि पुण्यदे ।
> नमस्ते नारदनुते नारायण मनःप्रिये ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> tulasi śrīsakhi śubhe pāpahāriṇi puṇyade ।
> namaste nāradanute nārāyaṇa manaḥpriye ॥

## English meaning

O Tulasī, friend of Śrī (Lakṣmī), auspicious one, remover of sins, giver of merit; salutations to you, praised by Nārada, beloved of the mind of Nārāyaṇa.

## Action while reciting

Perform namaskāra to the tulasī plant and apply tulasī *mṛttikā* (mud) to the forehead, then feed grass to the cows and perform namaskāra to the cows (chanting the next verse).
