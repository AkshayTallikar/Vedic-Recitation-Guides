# Kṛṣṇa Mantra — Oṃ Klīṃ Kṛṣṇāya Namaḥ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं क्लीं कृष्णाय नमः ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ klīṃ kṛṣṇāya namaḥ oṃ ।

## English meaning

Oṃ, klīṃ — salutations to Kṛṣṇa — Oṃ.

## Action while reciting

The Kṛṣṇa mantra, japa'd after its dhyāna; offer tarpaṇa "*oṃ klīṃ kṛṣṇāya namaḥ oṃ tarpayāmi*" once after every ten repetitions.
