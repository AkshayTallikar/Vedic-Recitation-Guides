# Uddhṛtāsi Varāheṇa (Mṛttikā mantra)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> उद्धृतासि वराहेण कृष्णेन शतबाहुना ।
> मृत्तिके हन मे पापं यन्मया दुष्कृतं कृतम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> uddhṛtāsi varāheṇa kṛṣṇena śatabāhunā ।
> mṛttike hana me pāpaṃ yanmayā duṣkṛtaṃ kṛtam ॥

## English meaning

O Bhūdevī (sacred earth), you were raised up by Kṛṣṇa in the form of the hundred-armed Boar (Varāha). O mṛttikā, destroy my sin — whatever wrong has been done by me.

## Action while reciting

Chanting this mantra, rub *mṛttikā* (sacred earth/clay) all over the body. Then utter loudly "*gaṅge māṃ punīhi*" (गङ्गे मां पुनीहि — "O Gaṅgā, purify me"). On Ekādaśī, arghya should be offered only to Lord Viṣṇu.
