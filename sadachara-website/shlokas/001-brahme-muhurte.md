# Brāhme Muhūrte

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय हरिं ध्यायेदतन्द्रितः ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> brāhme muhūrte cotthāya hariṃ dhyāyedatandritaḥ ॥

## English meaning

A devotee should arise at *brahmamuhūrta* — 96 minutes before sunrise — and, free of drowsiness, meditate on Lord Sri Hari.

## Action while reciting

Wake during *brahmamuhūrta* (the ~96 minutes before sunrise) and meditate on Sri Hari.

> *Note: 1 gaḷige = 24 minutes; 4 gaḷiges (96 minutes) before sunrise, depending on the rātrimāna, is brahmamuhūrta.*
