# Madhva Praṇāma — Yo Vipralambha

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> यो विप्रलम्भविपरीतमतिप्रभूतवादान्निरस्य कृतवान् भुवि तत्त्ववादम् ।
> सर्वेश्वरो हरिरिति प्रतिपादयन्तमानन्दतीर्थमुनिवर्यमहं नमामि ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> yo vipralambhaviparītamatiprabhūtavādānnirasya kṛtavān bhuvi tattvavādam ।
> sarveśvaro haririti pratipādayantamānandatīrthamunivaryamahaṃ namāmi ॥

## English meaning

He who, dispelling the prolific doctrines born of deluded and perverse intellects, established Tattvavāda (the philosophy of reality) upon the earth, and who demonstrated that Hari is the Lord of all — that foremost of sages, Ānandatīrtha (Madhvācārya), I salute.

## Action while reciting

Salutation to Śrī Madhvācārya (Ānandatīrtha), offered after the Nārāyaṇa dhyāna, before commencing the pūjā.
