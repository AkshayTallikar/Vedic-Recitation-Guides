# Brahmapāra Stotra

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ब्रह्मपारं मुने श्रोतुमिच्छामः परमं स्तवम् ।
> जपता कण्डुना देवो येनाराध्यत केशवः ॥१॥
> पारं परं विष्णुरपारपारः परः परेभ्यः परमार्थरूपी ।
> स ब्रह्मपारः परपारभूतः परः पराणामपि पारपारः ॥२॥
> स कारणं कारणतस्ततोऽपि तस्यापि हेतुः परहेतुहेतुः ।
> कार्येषु चैवं स ह कर्मकर्तृरूपैरशेषैरवतीह सर्वम् ॥३॥
> स ब्रह्मभूर्ब्रह्म स सर्वभूतो ब्रह्मप्रजानां पतिरच्युतोऽसौ ।
> ब्रह्माव्ययं नित्यमजं स विष्णुरपक्षयाद्यैरखिलैरसङ्गि ॥४॥
> ब्रह्माक्षरमजं नित्यं यथासौ पुरुषोत्तमः ।
> तथा रागादयो दोषाः प्रयान्तु प्रशमं मम ॥५॥
> एतद्वै ब्रह्मपाराख्यं संस्तवं परमं जपन् ।
> अवाप परमां सिद्धिं स समाराध्य केशवम् ॥६॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> brahmapāraṃ mune śrotumicchāmaḥ paramaṃ stavam ।
> japatā kaṇḍunā devo yenārādhyata keśavaḥ ॥1॥
> pāraṃ paraṃ viṣṇurapārapāraḥ paraḥ parebhyaḥ paramārtharūpī ।
> sa brahmapāraḥ parapārabhūtaḥ paraḥ parāṇāmapi pārapāraḥ ॥2॥
> sa kāraṇaṃ kāraṇatastato'pi tasyāpi hetuḥ parahetuhetuḥ ।
> kāryeṣu caivaṃ sa ha karmakartṛrūpairaśeṣairavatīha sarvam ॥3॥
> sa brahmabhūrbrahma sa sarvabhūto brahmaprajānāṃ patiracyuto'sau ।
> brahmāvyayaṃ nityamajaṃ sa viṣṇurapakṣayādyairakhilairasaṅgi ॥4॥
> brahmākṣaramajaṃ nityaṃ yathāsau puruṣottamaḥ ।
> tathā rāgādayo doṣāḥ prayāntu praśamaṃ mama ॥5॥
> etadvai brahmapārākhyaṃ saṃstavaṃ paramaṃ japan ।
> avāpa paramāṃ siddhiṃ sa samārādhya keśavam ॥6॥

## English meaning

(Pracetas said:) "O sage, we wish to hear that supreme hymn called *Brahmapāra*, by reciting which the sage Kaṇḍu propitiated Lord Keśava." (Soma replied:) Viṣṇu is the far shore beyond the far shore, supreme, beyond even the highest, the very form of the ultimate truth. He is the far shore of Brahman, become the far shore beyond every far shore, supreme, the very far shore of the highest things. He is the cause behind every cause, the source even of that source, the very ground of the highest ground. In all effects He, through the forms of every doer and deed without exception, pervades the whole right here. He is the become-Brahman who is Brahman itself, become all beings, the imperishable Lord of Brahmā's progeny; the undecaying, unborn, eternal Brahman — that Viṣṇu untouched by decay and every limitation. Just as He is the imperishable, unborn, eternal Puruṣottama — so may my faults, passion and the rest, come to quietude. Reciting this supreme hymn named Brahmapāra, he (Kaṇḍu), having worshipped Keśava, attained the highest perfection.

## Action while reciting

A stuti to Viṣṇu/Keśava recited during Lakṣmī pūjā (chanted, per the text, before the main pūjā begins, after sandhyāvandana and the offering of gandha/tulasī). The hymn plays throughout on *pāra/para* — "the supreme one beyond every shore." (Note: verses 2–4 are in the eleven-syllable triṣṭubh/upajāti metre; verses 1, 5, 6 in anuṣṭubh — split accordingly when rendering audio.)
