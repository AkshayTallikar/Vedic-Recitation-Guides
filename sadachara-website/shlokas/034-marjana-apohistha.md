# Mārjana — Āpo Hi Ṣṭhā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं आपो हि ष्ठा मयोभुवस्ता न ऊर्जे दधातन ।
> महे रणाय चक्षसे ।
> यो वः शिवतमो रसस्तस्य भाजयतेह नः ।
> उशतीरिव मातरः ।
> तस्मा अरं गमाम वो यस्य क्षयाय जिन्वथ ।
> आपो जनयथा च नः । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ āpo hi ṣṭhā mayobhuvastā na ūrje dadhātana ।
> mahe raṇāya cakṣase ।
> yo vaḥ śivatamo rasastasya bhājayateha naḥ ।
> uśatīriva mātaraḥ ।
> tasmā araṃ gamāma vo yasya kṣayāya jinvatha ।
> āpo janayathā ca naḥ । oṃ ।

## English meaning

O Waters, you are indeed the source of well-being; grant us nourishment, that we may behold great delight. Make us partake here of that most auspicious essence of yours, like loving mothers. May we come fully to that abode of yours for which you quicken us. O Waters, generate us anew.

## Action while reciting

The Mārjana — spraying water (taken in the spoon) over oneself with this Āpohiṣṭhā sūkta. Viniyoga: *āpohiṣṭheti tryarcasya sūktasya āmbarīṣa-sindhudvīpa ṛṣiḥ; gāyatrī chandaḥ; āpo devatā; mārjane viniyogaḥ.*
