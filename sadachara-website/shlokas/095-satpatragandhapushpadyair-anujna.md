# Satpatragandhapuṣpādyair (Anujñā)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> सत्पत्रगन्धपुष्पाद्यैरर्चयामि यथा हरे ।
> अनुज्ञां देहि देवेश संसारार्तिनिवृत्तये ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> satpatragandhapuṣpādyairarcayāmi yathā hare ।
> anujñāṃ dehi deveśa saṃsārārtinivṛttaye ॥

## English meaning

O Hari, as I worship You with sacred leaves, sandal, flowers and the rest, grant me, O Lord of gods, Your permission — for the cessation of the affliction of saṃsāra.

## Action while reciting

The anujñā (seeking the Lord's leave) recited just before beginning the pūjā with leaves, gandha and flowers, praying to cross the ocean of worldly suffering.
