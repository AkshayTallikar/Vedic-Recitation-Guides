# Lakṣmīnārāyaṇaṃ Vande

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> लक्ष्मीनारायणं वन्दे तद्भक्तप्रवरो हि यः ।
> श्रीमदानन्दतीर्थाख्यो गुरुस्तं च नमाम्यहम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> lakṣmīnārāyaṇaṃ vande tadbhaktapravaro hi yaḥ ।
> śrīmadānandatīrthākhyo gurustaṃ ca namāmyaham ॥

## English meaning

I bow to Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa; and he who is His foremost devotee — the preceptor named Śrīmad Ānandatīrtha (Madhvācārya) — to that guru also I offer salutation.

## Action while reciting

The guru-vandana verse of the Hariguru-dhyāna, saluting Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa and the guru Madhvācārya.
