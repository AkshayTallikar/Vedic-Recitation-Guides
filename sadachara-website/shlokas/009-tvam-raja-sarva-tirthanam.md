# Tvaṃ Rājā Sarva Tīrthānāṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> त्वं राजा सर्व तीर्थानां त्वमेव जगतः पिता ।
> याचितं देहि मे तीर्थं सर्वपापप्रमोचनम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> tvaṃ rājā sarva tīrthānāṃ tvameva jagataḥ pitā ।
> yācitaṃ dehi me tīrthaṃ sarvapāpapramocanam ॥

## English meaning

You are the king of all sacred waters (tīrthas); you alone are the father of the world. Grant me, as I beg, the holy water that releases from all sins.

## Action while reciting

To begin the bath, first chant *tīrtharājāya namaḥ* and pay obeisance to Lord Varuṇa, then recite this verse.
