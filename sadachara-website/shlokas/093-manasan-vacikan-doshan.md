# Mānasān Vācikān Doṣān

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> मानसान् वाचिकान् दोषान् कायिकानपि सर्वदा ।
> वैष्णवद्वेषसंहेतून्मे भस्मसात् कुरु माधव ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> mānasān vācikān doṣān kāyikānapi sarvadā ।
> vaiṣṇavadveṣasaṃhetūnme bhasmasāt kuru mādhava ॥

## English meaning

O Mādhava, reduce to ashes all my faults — of mind, of speech, and of body — that ever arise as enmity toward the Vaiṣṇavas (devotees of the Lord).

## Action while reciting

A prayer to Madhva (Mādhava) to expiate one's sins, especially wrong thoughts, words and deeds against the Lord's devotees, recited as part of the guru-vandana.
