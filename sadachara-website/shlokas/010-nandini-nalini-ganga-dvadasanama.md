# Nandinī Nalinī — Twelve Names of Gaṅgā

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नन्दिनी नलिनी सीता मालती च मलापहा ।
> विष्णुपादाब्जसम्भूता गङ्गा त्रिपथगामिनि ॥
> भागीरथी भोगवती जाह्नवी त्रिदशेश्वरी ।
> द्वादशैतानि नामानि यत्र यत्र जलाशये ॥
> स्नानकाले पठेन्नित्यं तत्र तत्र वसाम्यहम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> nandinī nalinī sītā mālatī ca malāpahā ।
> viṣṇupādābjasambhūtā gaṅgā tripathagāmini ॥
> bhāgīrathī bhogavatī jāhnavī tridaśeśvarī ।
> dvādaśaitāni nāmāni yatra yatra jalāśaye ॥
> snānakāle paṭhennityaṃ tatra tatra vasāmyaham ॥

## English meaning

Nandinī, Nalinī, Sītā, Mālatī, Malāpahā (remover of impurity), Viṣṇupādābja-sambhūtā (born of the lotus-foot of Viṣṇu), Gaṅgā, Tripathagāminī (flowing in three paths), Bhāgīrathī, Bhogavatī, Jāhnavī, Tridaśeśvarī — "[Gaṅgā says:] wherever, in whatever body of water, one daily recites these twelve names of mine at the time of bathing, there I am present."

## Action while reciting

If these twelve names of Gaṅgā are chanted, Gaṅgā becomes present in the bathing water. When bathing in a river, stand facing the direction from which the water flows; the water should reach up to the navel. Unloose the tuft of hair, hold the breath, and dip the head and then the back three times.

> *Ritual notes from this section: One should not bathe in fire-heated water when healthy — `वह्निसप्ताम्बुना स्नातो नाप्नोति स्नानजं फलम्` (vahnitaptāmbunā snāto nāpnoti snānajaṃ phalam: "one who bathes in hot water does not obtain the merit of the bath"). If ill and forced to use hot water, fill the bucket with a little cold water, then hot, then cold again, and bathe while touching the water and chanting "gaṅge ca yamune caiva…". If seriously ill, rub the body with a wet cloth, perform ācamana, and sprinkle water on the body chanting the "āpohiṣṭhā…" mantra.*
