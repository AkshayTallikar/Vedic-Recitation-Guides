# Hare Rāma Hare Kṛṣṇa (Tāraka Mantra)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> हरे राम हरे राम राम राम हरे हरे ।
> हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare ।
> hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare ॥

## English meaning

The Tāraka (liberating) Mahāmantra invoking the names of Hari as Rāma and Kṛṣṇa.

## Action while reciting

Chant on rising, following the Govinda waking verse.
