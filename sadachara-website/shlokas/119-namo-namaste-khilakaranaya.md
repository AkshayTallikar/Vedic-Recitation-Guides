# Namo Namaste'khilakāraṇāya

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नमो नमस्तेऽखिलकारणाय नारायणायार्तिविनाशनाय ।
> सर्वागमाम्नाय महार्णवाय नमोऽपवर्गाय परायणाय ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> namo namaste'khilakāraṇāya nārāyaṇāyārtivināśanāya ।
> sarvāgamāmnāya mahārṇavāya namo'pavargāya parāyaṇāya ॥

## English meaning

Salutations, salutations to You — the cause of all, to Nārāyaṇa the destroyer of affliction; to the very tradition and canon of all scriptures, the great ocean; to the giver of liberation, the supreme refuge — salutations.

## Action while reciting

A salutation to Nārāyaṇa within the closing namaskāra-stuti, hailing Him as the cause of all and the bestower of liberation (apavarga).
