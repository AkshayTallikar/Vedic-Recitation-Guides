# Āgamārthaṃ Tu Devānāṃ (Ghaṇṭā)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> आगमार्थं तु देवानां गमनार्थं तु रक्षसाम् ।
> कुरु घण्टारवं तत्र देवताह्वानलाञ्छनम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> āgamārthaṃ tu devānāṃ gamanārthaṃ tu rakṣasām ।
> kuru ghaṇṭāravaṃ tatra devatāhvānalāñchanam ॥

## English meaning

For the coming of the gods and for the departure of the demons, ring the bell there — the sign that summons the deities.

## Action while reciting

Recited while ringing the bell (ghaṇṭā) at the start of pūjā, to welcome the deities and drive away malign forces.
