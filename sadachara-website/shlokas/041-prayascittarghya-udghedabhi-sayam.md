# Prāyaścittārghya — Udghedabhi (evening)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं उद्घेदभि श्रुतामघं वृषभं नर्यापसम् ।
> अस्तारमेषि सूर्य । ओं ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ udghedabhi śrutāmaghaṃ vṛṣabhaṃ naryāpasam ।
> astārameṣi sūrya । oṃ ।

## English meaning

Rise up towards the famed, bounteous, manly, beneficent one (Sūrya); O Sūrya, you reach the setting place (the western home of day).

## Action while reciting

The evening (sāyaṅkāla) Prāyaścittārghya; offer one arghya with this mantra. Viniyoga: *udghedabhītyasya mantrasya sukakṣa ṛṣiḥ; gāyatrī chandaḥ; agnirdevatā; sāyaṃsandhyākālātīta-doṣaparihārārtham prāyaścittārghyapradāne viniyogaḥ.*

> *Note from the text: when one has not learned to utter a Veda mantra with the proper intonation (svara), it may be uttered like an ordinary stotra mantra.*
