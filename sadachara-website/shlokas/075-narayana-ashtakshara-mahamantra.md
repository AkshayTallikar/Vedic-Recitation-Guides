# Nārāyaṇa Aṣṭākṣara Mahāmantra

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं नमो नारायणाय ।

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya ।

## English meaning

Oṃ. Salutations to Nārāyaṇa.

## Action while reciting

The eight-syllable (aṣṭākṣara) Mahāmantra of Śrī Nārāyaṇa, japa'd after the pañcāṅganyāsa. Viniyoga: *asya śrīnārāyaṇāṣṭākṣara-mahāmantrasya antaryāmī ṛṣiḥ; daivī gāyatrī chandaḥ; śrīnārāyaṇo devatā.* (The pañcāṅganyāsa assigns the bīja forms — "*oṃ kṛddholkāya hṛdayāya namaḥ; oṃ maholkāya śirase svāhā…*" — to the limbs before the japa.)
