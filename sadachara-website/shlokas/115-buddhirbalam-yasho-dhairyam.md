# Buddhirbalaṃ Yaśo Dhairyaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> बुद्धिर्बलं यशो धैर्यं निर्भयत्वमरोगता ।
> अजाड्यं वाक्पटुत्वं च हनूमत्स्मरणाद्भवेत् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> buddhirbalaṃ yaśo dhairyaṃ nirbhayatvamarogatā ।
> ajāḍyaṃ vākpaṭutvaṃ ca hanūmatsmaraṇādbhavet ॥

## English meaning

Intelligence, strength, fame, courage, fearlessness, freedom from disease, alertness of mind, and eloquence of speech — all these arise from remembering Hanumān.

## Action while reciting

Recited together with the previous verse as a salutation to Hanumān, declaring the fruits of his remembrance.
