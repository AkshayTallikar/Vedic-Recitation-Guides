# Ye Ke Cāsmatkule Jātā (Keśaniṣpīḍana tarpaṇa)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ये के चास्मत्कुले जाता अपुत्रा गोत्रिणो मृताः ।
> ते गृह्णन्तु मया दत्तं केशनिष्पीडनोदकम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> ye ke cāsmatkule jātā aputrā gotriṇo mṛtāḥ ।
> te gṛhṇantu mayā dattaṃ keśaniṣpīḍanodakam ॥

## English meaning

Whoever in our family have been born and died sonless, of our gotra — may they accept this water, wrung from my (wet) hair and offered by me.

## Action while reciting

Offer the water squeezed from the wet hair (*keśaniṣpīḍana*) as tarpaṇa to ancestors who died without sons. (Preceding tarpaṇa: facing east, offer one tarpaṇa to the devatās of the three worlds from the fingertips; shifting the yajñopavīta over the chest and facing north, offer two to the ṛṣis from the base of the little finger; shifting it to the right shoulder and facing south, offer three to the pitṛs from the base of the thumb.)
