# Brahmakamaṇḍalu-sambhūte (Arghya to Gaṅgā)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ब्रह्मकमण्डलुसम्भूते पूर्णचन्द्रनिभानने ।
> त्रैलोक्यवन्दिते गङ्गे गृहाणार्घ्यं नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> brahmakamaṇḍalusambhūte pūrṇacandranibhānane ।
> trailokyavandite gaṅge gṛhāṇārghyaṃ namo'stu te ॥

## English meaning

O Gaṅgā, born from the water-pot of Brahmā, whose face resembles the full moon, revered by the three worlds — accept this arghya, salutations to you.

## Action while reciting

Offer arghya to Gaṅgā with this mantra. While bathing in a river, chant the river's *abhimānidevatā* mantra; if not known, utter the river's name and say "*tadantaryāmi nārāyaṇa idam arghyam*" (e.g. *kāveryantargata nārāyaṇa…*).
