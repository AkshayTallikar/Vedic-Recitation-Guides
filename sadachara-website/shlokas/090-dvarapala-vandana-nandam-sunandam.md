# Dvārapāla Vandana — Nandaṃ Sunandaṃ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नन्दं सुनन्दं गरुडं प्रचण्डं चण्डमेव च ।
> जयं च विजयं चैव कुमुदं कुमुदेक्षणम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> nandaṃ sunandaṃ garuḍaṃ pracaṇḍaṃ caṇḍameva ca ।
> jayaṃ ca vijayaṃ caiva kumudaṃ kumudekṣaṇam ॥

## English meaning

(Salutations to) Nanda, Sunanda, Garuḍa, Pracaṇḍa and Caṇḍa, Jaya and Vijaya, Kumuda and Kumudekṣaṇa.

## Action while reciting

The Dvārapāla-vandanam — salutation to the eight gatekeepers of Viṣṇu's abode (Vaikuṇṭha), offered along with worship of Śrī-Lakṣmī at the threshold before entering the main pūjā. (Preceded by 'śrī śriyai namaḥ'.)
