# Śaṅkha Stuti — Tvaṃ Purā Sāgarotpanno

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> त्वं पुरा सागरोत्पन्नो विष्णुना विधृतः करे ।
> नमितः सर्वदेवैश्च पाञ्चजन्य नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
> गर्भादेवारिनारीणां विशीर्यन्ते सहस्रधा ।
> तव नादेन पाताले पाञ्चजन्य नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
> दर्शनादेव शङ्खस्य किं पुनः स्पर्शनेन च ।
> विलयं यान्ति पापानि तमः सूर्योदये यथा ॥
> नत्वा शङ्खं करे स्पृष्ट्वा मन्त्रेणानेन वैष्णवः ।
> यः स्नापयति गोविन्दं तस्य पुण्यमनन्तकम् ॥
> शङ्खं चन्द्रार्कदैवत्यं मध्ये वरुणदैवतम् ।
> पृष्ठे प्रजापतिर्वेदा अग्रे गङ्गा सरस्वती ॥
> त्रैलोक्ये यानि तीर्थानि वासुदेवस्य चाज्ञया ।
> शङ्खे तिष्ठन्ति विप्रेन्द्र तस्माच्छङ्खं प्रपूजयेत् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> tvaṃ purā sāgarotpanno viṣṇunā vidhṛtaḥ kare ।
> namitaḥ sarvadevaiśca pāñcajanya namo'stu te ॥
> garbhādevārinārīṇāṃ viśīryante sahasradhā ।
> tava nādena pātāle pāñcajanya namo'stu te ॥
> darśanādeva śaṅkhasya kiṃ punaḥ sparśanena ca ।
> vilayaṃ yānti pāpāni tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā ॥
> natvā śaṅkhaṃ kare spṛṣṭvā mantreṇānena vaiṣṇavaḥ ।
> yaḥ snāpayati govindaṃ tasya puṇyamanantakam ॥
> śaṅkhaṃ candrārkadaivatyaṃ madhye varuṇadaivatam ।
> pṛṣṭhe prajāpatirvedā agre gaṅgā sarasvatī ॥
> trailokye yāni tīrthāni vāsudevasya cājñayā ।
> śaṅkhe tiṣṭhanti viprendra tasmācchaṅkhaṃ prapūjayet ॥

## English meaning

O Pāñcajanya, you were born of old from the ocean and are held in Viṣṇu's hand; bowed to by all the gods — salutations to you. At your sound in the netherworld the wombs of the demon-women miscarry a thousandfold; O Pāñcajanya, salutations to you. If the mere sight of the divine conch (destroys sin), how much more its touch! Sins dissolve as darkness does at sunrise. When a Vaiṣṇava, having bowed and touched the conch with this mantra, bathes Govinda (with its water), his merit is endless. The conch has the sun and moon as its presiding deities, Varuṇa in its middle, Prajāpati and the Vedas at its back, and Gaṅgā and Sarasvatī at its mouth. All the sacred fords (tīrthas) of the three worlds, by Vāsudeva's command, abide within the conch — therefore, O best of brahmins, one should worship the conch.

## Action while reciting

The Śaṅkha-stuti, recited during śaṅkhapūjā while honoring and filling the conch with water for the Lord's abhiṣeka.
