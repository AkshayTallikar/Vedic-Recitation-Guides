# Oṃ Namo Bhagavate — Three Dip Mantras

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं ओं नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ओं ।
> ओं ओं नमो नारायणाय ओं ।
> ओं विष्णवे नमः ओं ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ oṃ namo bhagavate vāsudevāya oṃ ।
> oṃ oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya oṃ ।
> oṃ viṣṇave namaḥ oṃ ॥

## English meaning

Oṃ, salutations to the Lord Vāsudeva; Oṃ, salutations to Nārāyaṇa; Oṃ, salutations to Viṣṇu.

## Action while reciting

Chanting these three mantras, take a dip three times (one per mantra). Then, chanting the "*āpohiṣṭhā…*" mantra, sprinkle water on the body.
