# Namaḥ Kamalanābhāya (Arghya to Viṣṇu)

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> नमः कमलनाभाय नमस्ते जलशायिने ।
> नमस्तेऽस्तु हृषीकेश गृहाणार्घ्यं नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> namaḥ kamalanābhāya namaste jalaśāyine ।
> namaste'stu hṛṣīkeśa gṛhāṇārghyaṃ namo'stu te ॥

## English meaning

Salutations to the lotus-naveled one, salutations to the one who reclines upon the waters; salutations to you, O Hṛṣīkeśa — accept this arghya, salutations to you.

## Action while reciting

Chant the *dvādaśanāma* (Keśava, Nārāyaṇa, etc.), invoke the presence of *gopīcandana* in the water, and apply the twelve nāmas on the body. Then, chanting this mantra, offer arghya (water oblation) to Lord Viṣṇu.
