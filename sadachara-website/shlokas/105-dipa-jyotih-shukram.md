# Dīpa — Jyotiḥ Śukraṃ Tejasvī

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ज्योतिः शुक्रं तेजस्वी देवानां सततं प्रियः ।
> प्रभाकरो महातेजा दीपोऽयं प्रतिगृह्यताम् ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> jyotiḥ śukraṃ tejasvī devānāṃ satataṃ priyaḥ ।
> prabhākaro mahātejā dīpo'yaṃ pratigṛhyatām ॥

## English meaning

Bright, pure and radiant, ever dear to the gods, luminous and of great brilliance — may this lamp be accepted.

## Action while reciting

Recited while offering dīpa (the lamp) to the Lord. (The lamp is shown but not waved as āratī here, and is snuffed out by the worshipper, not by breath.)
