# Nārāyaṇa Dhyāna — Udyadbhāsvatsamābhāsaḥ

## Sanskrit (Devanagari)

> ओं उद्यद्भास्वत्समाभासश्चिदानन्दैकदेहवान् ।
> चक्रशङ्खगदापद्मधरो ध्येयोऽहमीश्वरः ॥
> लक्ष्मीधराभ्यामाश्लिष्टः स्वमूर्तिगणमध्यगः ।
> ब्रह्मवायुशिवाहीशविपैः शक्रादिकैरपि ॥
> सेव्यमानोऽधिकं भक्त्या नित्यनिःशेषशक्तिमान् ।
> मूर्तयोऽष्टावपि ध्येयाश्चक्रशङ्खवराभयैः ॥
> युक्ताः प्रदीपवर्णाश्च सर्वाभरणभूषिताः ।
> बिम्बोऽसि प्रतिबिम्बोऽस्मि तव यद्यपि चान्तरम् ॥
> स्वामिन् निर्दोष मद्दोषं विरेचय नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

## Transliteration (IAST)

> oṃ udyadbhāsvatsamābhāsaścidānandaikadehavān ।
> cakraśaṅkhagadāpadmadharo dhyeyo'hamīśvaraḥ ॥
> lakṣmīdharābhyāmāśliṣṭaḥ svamūrtigaṇamadhyagaḥ ।
> brahmavāyuśivāhīśavipaiḥ śakrādikairapi ॥
> sevyamāno'dhikaṃ bhaktyā nityaniḥśeṣaśaktimān ।
> mūrtayo'ṣṭāvapi dhyeyāścakraśaṅkhavarābhayaiḥ ॥
> yuktāḥ pradīpavarṇāśca sarvābharaṇabhūṣitāḥ ।
> bimbo'si pratibimbo'smi tava yadyapi cāntaram ॥
> svāmin nirdoṣa maddoṣaṃ virecaya namo'stu te ॥

## English meaning

"I, the Lord, whose radiance resembles the rising sun, whose single body is consciousness-and-bliss, bearing discus, conch, mace and lotus, am to be meditated upon." Embraced by Lakṣmī and Bhū (Dharā), abiding amid the host of His own forms, served with abounding devotion by Brahmā, Vāyu, Śiva, Ahīśa (Śeṣa, lord of serpents) and others including Śakra (Indra) — ever full of unlimited power. His eight forms too are to be meditated upon, each bearing discus, conch, boon-gesture and abhaya, lamp-bright and adorned with every ornament. You are the original (bimba), I am the reflection (pratibimba); though I am ever distinct from You and Your servant. O Lord, flawless one, wash away my faults — salutations to You.

## Action while reciting

The dhyāna recited before the Nārāyaṇa aṣṭākṣara japa, meditating on the Lord and His eight forms (the bimba–pratibimba contemplation).
