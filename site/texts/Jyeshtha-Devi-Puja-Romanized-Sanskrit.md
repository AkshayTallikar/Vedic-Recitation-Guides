# Jyeṣṭhā Devī Pūjā: Romanized Sanskrit Mantras & English Translation

**Source:** *Jyesta Devi Puja 2026_Kan.pdf*, Vishva Madhwa Maha Parishat Granthamala 259, pp. 3–12.  
**Compiler:** Pt. Mahidasa Acharya Joshi, Satti  
**Publisher:** Śrīman Madhvācārya Mūla Mahāsaṃsthānam, Śrī Uttarādi Matha / Vishva Madhwa Maha Parishat Research Center, Bangalore.

**Review note:** The Sanskrit follows the printed booklet, including its nonstandard `sadgati-prakṛti` and `araṇyām` readings. The fire-stick comparison in the invocation's meaning is an interpretation of the intended analogy. Ellipses in the saṅkalpa are present in the source. English ritual directions and the vrata narrative are separate from the Sanskrit recitation.

---

## 1. Vrata Paricaya & Kāla Vicāra (Introduction & Timing)
*(Source: p. 3)*

- **Tithi & Nakṣatra:** Bhādrapada Śukla Pakṣa Aṣṭamī tithi in conjunction with Jyeṣṭhā Nakṣatra.
- **Trirātra Krama (Three-Night Observance):**
  - **Anurādhā Nakṣatra:** *Āvāhana* (Invocation / welcoming the goddess)
  - **Jyeṣṭhā Nakṣatra:** *Mukhya Pūjā* (Main worship)
  - **Mūlā Nakṣatra:** *Visarjana* (Formal conclusion / farewell)
  *According to the Shastras, this three-day observance is the most auspicious method (Uttama Kalpa).*
- **Phalaśruti (Spiritual Fruits & Benefits):** Alleviation of poverty (*dāridrya-parihāra*), unbroken marital auspiciousness and prosperity (*akhaṇḍa-saubhāgya*), noble progeny (*santāna-prāpti*), and the fulfillment of all cherished desires for the family (*sarvābhīṣṭa-siddhi*).

## 2. Saṅkalpa (Solemn Vow of Worship)
*(Source: p. 3)*

### Sanskrit (IAST):
> ācamya, prāṇān āyamya, śubhe śobhane muhūrte, śrīgovinda govinda ... asmin vartamāne śrīparameṣṭhinaḥ dvitīyaparārdhe ... asmin vartamāne śubhasaṃvatsare, dakṣiṇāyane, varṣaṛtau, bhādrapada-māse, śuklapakṣe, aṣṭamyāṃ śubhatithau, budha/bṛhaspati-vāsare, jyeṣṭhā-nakṣatra-yuktāyāṃ, śubhayoga-śubhakaraṇa-evaṃguṇa-viśeṣaṇa-viśiṣṭāyāṃ śubhatithau:
>
> mameha janmani janmāntare ca akhaṇḍa-saubhāgya, putra-pautrādi-aiśvarya, sadgati-prakṛti-sarvābhīṣṭa-siddhy-arthaṃ, śrī-jyeṣṭhālakṣmī-devy-antargata śrīnārāyaṇa-preraṇayā śrīnārāyaṇa-prīty-arthaṃ jyeṣṭhālakṣmī-vrata-kalpokta-pūjāṃ kariṣye ||

### English Meaning:
> Having performed purification through sipping water (*ācamana*) and regulated the vital breath (*prāṇāyāma*), on this auspicious, glorious occasion, remembering the holy name of Lord Govinda, Govinda... during the present second half of the lifespan of Lord Brahma (Parameṣṭhin)... in this ongoing auspicious year, in the southern solstitial half of the year (*dakṣiṇāyana*), in the rainy season (*varṣā-ṛtu*), in the month of Bhādrapada, in the bright fortnight (*śukla-pakṣa*), on the auspicious eighth lunar day (*aṣṭamī*), on Wednesday / Thursday, conjoined with the constellation Jyeṣṭhā, endowed with all auspicious astrological yogas and karaṇas:
>
> In this lifetime and in future lifetimes, for obtaining uninterrupted fortune and auspiciousness (*akhaṇḍa-saubhāgya*), the wealth of noble children and grandchildren, spiritual elevation, and the fulfillment of all cherished aspirations, by the inspiration of Supreme Lord Śrī Nārāyaṇa indwelling Goddess Śrī Jyeṣṭhā Lakṣmī, and solely for the pleasure and grace of Śrī Nārāyaṇa, I hereby perform the worship ordained in the Jyeṣṭhā Lakṣmī Vrata ritual manual (*kalpa*).

## 3. Śaṅkha Pūjā (Worship of the Sacred Conch)
*(Source: p. 4)*

*(Ring the ceremonial bell — ghaṇṭānādaṃ kuryāt)*

### Purification:
> sahasrāra huṃ phaṭ  
> *(iti śaṅkhaṃ prakṣālya)*  
> *Meaning: (Purifying and washing the holy conch with the Astra mantra)*

### Verse 1:
> tvaṃ purā sāgarotpanno viṣṇunā vidhṛtaḥ kare |  
> namitaḥ sarvadevaiś ca pāñcajanya namo'stu te || 1 ||

**Meaning:** You arose of old from the great ocean, and are held aloft in the sacred hand of Lord Viṣṇu. Revered and bowed down to by all the celestials, O sacred conch Pāñcajanya, salutations unto you!

### Verse 2:
> garbhā devāri-nārīṇāṃ viśīryante sahasradhā |  
> tava nādena pātāle pāñcajanya namo'stu te || 2 ||

**Meaning:** By your thunderous, resonant blast resounding down to the netherworlds (*pātāla*), the wombs of the spouses of the demons (the enemies of the gods) are shattered into a thousand pieces. O Pāñcajanya, salutations unto you!

### Verse 3:
> darśanād eva śaṅkhasya kiṃ punaḥ sparśane kṛte |  
> vilayaṃ yānti pāpāni tamaḥ sūryodaye yathā || 3 ||

**Meaning:** Merely by having sight (*darśana*) of the conch, let alone touching it, all sins dissolve away into nothingness, just as pervasive darkness vanishes at the rising of the radiant sun!

### Verse 4:
> natvā śaṅkhaṃ kare spṛṣṭvā mantreṇānena vaiṣṇavaḥ |  
> yaḥ snāpayati govindaṃ tasya puṇyam anantakam || 4 ||

**Meaning:** The devotee of Viṣṇu (*vaiṣṇava*) who bows to the conch, touches it with their hands, recites this mantra, and bathes Lord Govinda with its sanctified water earns boundless, infinite merit (*puṇya*).

*(Offer holy tulasī, sandalwood paste, and flowers to the conch, and sprinkle its holy water on yourself and the ritual items.)*

## 6. Āvāhana (Invocation of the Goddess)
*(Source: p. 5)*

### Sanskrit (IAST):
> svātmasaṃsthām ajāṃ śuddhāṃ tvām adya parameśvari |  
> araṇyām iva havyāśaṃ mūrtāv āvāhayāmy aham ||
>
> saśrīkaṃ saparivāraṃ jyeṣṭhālakṣmy-antargata-śrīlakṣmīnārāyaṇaṃ  
> āgacchāgaccha āvāhayāmi ||

### English Meaning:
> O Supreme Goddess (*Parameśvarī*), though You are unborn (*ajā*), eternally pure (*śuddhā*), and established in Your own being (*svātma-saṃsthā*), I now invoke You into this consecrated form, just as fire (*havyāśa*) latent within churning-wood (*araṇi*) is made manifest.
>
> Endowed with all divine glory and surrounded by Your divine retinue, O Lord Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa dwelling within Goddess Jyeṣṭhā Lakṣmī: please come, please come! I invoke and welcome You!

## 7. Ṣoḍaśopacāra Pūjā (The Sixteen Royal Offerings)
*(Source: pp. 5–8)*

### 1. Dhyānam (Meditation)
> devānāṃ devatānāṃ ca jyeṣṭhādevi tvam ādimā |  
> ādiśakte namas te'stu namas te viṣṇuvallabhe ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | dhyānaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Among gods and goddesses, O Goddess Jyeṣṭhā, You are the primordial, elder sovereign. O Primordial Energy (*Ādiśakti*), salutations unto You! Salutations to You, the beloved consort of Lord Viṣṇu! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer this meditation.

---

### 2. Āvāhanam (Invocation & Welcome)
> brahmapriye namas te'stu namas te bhaktavatsale |  
> viṣṇupriye namas te'stu jagadānandakāriṇi ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | āvāhanaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Salutations to You who are dear to Brahman; salutations to You who are affectionate to Your devotees! Salutations to You, beloved of Viṣṇu; salutations to You who grant delight to the entire cosmos! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer this invocation.

---

### 3. Āsanam (Offering the Sacred Seat)
> āsane svarṇaratnāḍhye sarvaśobhāsamanvite |  
> upaviśya jaganmātaḥ prasādābhimukhī bhava ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | āsanaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Gracing this splendid seat adorned with fine gold, luminous gems, and all exquisite decorations, be pleased to be seated, O Mother of the Universe, and turn Your compassionate, graceful countenance toward us! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer this throne.

---

### 4. Pādyam (Water for Washing the Divine Lotus Feet)
> idaṃ gaṅgājalaṃ samyak suvarṇakalaśe sthitam |  
> pādyārthaṃ te prayacchāmi kṣālayāmi padāmbuje ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | pādyaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** This sacred, pure water of the river Gaṅgā, kept in a golden pitcher, I offer unto You for washing Your feet; I reverently bathe Your divine lotus feet. Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer water for the feet.

---

### 5. Arghyam (Offering of Sanctified Water)
> gandhapuṣpākṣatair yuktaṃ toyena ca samanvitam |  
> suvarṇapātre cārvaṅgi gṛhāṇārghyaṃ namo'stu te ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | arghyaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Blended with fragrant sandalpaste, sacred flowers, unbroken sanctified rice (*akṣatā*), and pure water, held in a golden vessel, O You of lovely, flawless form (*cārvaṅgī*), please accept this arghya offering. Salutations unto You!

---

### 6. Ācamanam (Water for Sipping)
> karpūrailāmṛgamadaiḥ suvāsair upaśobhitam |  
> gṛhāṇācamanīyaṃ ca śiśiraṃ vimalaṃ jalam ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | ācamanaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Fragrant and enhanced with refined camphor, green cardamom, and rich musk, please accept this cool, pristine, pure water for ceremonial sipping (*ācamana*). Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer water for sipping.

---

### 7. Madhuparkam (Offering of Honey-Yogurt Nectar)
> dadhyājyamadhusaṃmiśraṃ madhuparkaṃ sumañjulam |  
> devīnām api devi tvaṃ gṛhāṇābhīṣṭadāyini ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | madhuparkaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Prepared from a harmonious blend of curd/yogurt, clarified butter (ghee), and pure honey—this delightfully sweet *madhuparka*—O Goddess supreme even among all divinities, fulfiller of all wishes, please accept it! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer madhuparka.

---

### 8. Punarācamanam (Second Sipping Water)
> nadīnadasamudbhūtaṃ pavitraṃ nirmalaṃ jalam |  
> punarācamanaṃ devi gṛhāṇa jagadambike ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | punarācamanīyaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Originating from sacred rivers and pristine holy streams, pure and clear, please accept this water for sipping once again, O Divine Mother of the Universe! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer this second water for sipping.

---

### 9. Pañcāmṛta Snānam (The Five-Nectar Holy Bath)
> pañcāmṛtena snapanaṃ kariṣye bhaktavatsale |  
> payo dadhi ghṛtaṃ caiva mākṣikaṃ śarkarāyutam ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | pañcāmṛtasnānaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** O Loving Protector of devotees, I perform Your ceremonial holy bath with the five sacred nectars: fresh milk, curd, ghee, wild honey, and refined sugar. Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer the pañcāmṛta bath.

---

### 10. Śuddhodaka Snānam (Pure Water Ablution)
> mandākinyā samānītaṃ hemāmbhoruhavāsitam |  
> snānārthaṃ te mayā dattaṃ gṛhāṇa jagadambike ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | śuddhodakasnānaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Brought forth from the celestial Gaṅgā (Mandākinī), scented with golden lotus blossoms, this pure water is offered by me for Your bath; please accept it, O Mother of the Universe! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer the pure water bath.

---

### 11. Vastra & Upavastra (Divine Garments & Upper Cloth)
> kauśeyavasanaṃ divyaṃ kañcukena samanvitam |  
> upavastreṇa saṃvītaṃ gṛhāṇa parameśvari ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | vastropavastrāṇi samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Please accept these exquisite divine silk garments (*kauśeya-vasana*), accompanied by an auspicious blouse/bodice (*kañcuka*) and draped with an upper cloth (*upavastra*), O Supreme Goddess! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer sacred garments and vestments.

---

### 12. Gandham (Sacred Sandalwood Paste)
> imaṃ gandhaṃ mahaddivyaṃ kuṅkumena samanvitam |  
> vilepanārthaṃ te dattaṃ gṛhāṇa varadā bhava ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | gandhaṃ samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** This extraordinarily divine sandalwood paste, enriched with sacred red vermilion (*kuṅkuma*), is offered for anointing Your divine form; please accept it and bestow Your boons upon us! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer sacred fragrant sandalpaste.

---

### 13. Saubhāgya Dravyāṇi (Auspicious Ornaments & Cosmetics)
> kajjalaṃ caiva sindūraṃ haridrākuṅkumāni ca |  
> bhaktyā mayārpitānīśe jyeṣṭhāgauri gṛhāṇa bho ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | haridrākuṅkuma-saubhāgyadravyāṇi samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Eye collyrium (*kajjala*), red vermilion (*sindūra*), sacred turmeric (*haridrā*), and kuṅkuma—all these tokens of marital auspiciousness and fortune offered by me with heartfelt devotion, O Sovereign Lady, O Jyeṣṭhā Gauri, please accept them! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer turmeric, vermilion, and all auspicious items.

---

### 14. Puṣpāṇi (Offering of Flowers)
> nānāvidhāni mālyāni sugandhīni haripriye |  
> jyeṣṭhāgauri gṛhāṇa tvaṃ prasādābhimukhī bhava ||  
> **jyeṣṭhādevyai namaḥ | puṣpāṇi samarpayāmi ||**

**Meaning:** Diverse varieties of fragrant flower garlands, delightfully scented, O beloved consort of Lord Hari, O Jyeṣṭhā Gauri, please accept them and shower Your gracious favor upon us! Salutations to Goddess Jyeṣṭhā; I offer holy flowers.

## 10. Uttarapūjā, Naivedya & Nīrājana (Concluding Offerings)
*(Source: p. 10)*

### Dhūpam (Incense):
> vanaspatirasodbhūtaṃ nānāgandhasamanvitam |  
> daśāṅgasahitaṃ divyaṃ dhūpaṃ devi gṛhāṇa bho ||

**Meaning:** Formed from the pure sap and essences of aromatic trees and herbs, combined with various delightful fragrances and the ten traditional auspicious herbal ingredients (*daśāṅga*), please accept this divine incense offering, O Goddess!

---

## 13. Saṅkṣipta Vratakathā Sāra (Summary of the Sacred Vrata Narrative)
*(Source: pp. 11–12)*

### Background (Hinnele):
In the celestial realm of Kailāsa, Goddess Pārvatī respectfully asked Lord Śiva to reveal a sacred vrata that bestows upon women all enduring marital auspiciousness (*saubhāgya*), virtuous progeny (*santāna*), and the complete eradication of poverty and adversity (*dāridrya-parihāra*). In response, Lord Śiva extolled the unmatched glory and efficacy of the **Jyeṣṭhā Lakṣmī Vrata**. On earth, eminent sages assembled on the sacred banks of the Gaṅgā propagated its liturgical procedure for the upliftment of humanity.

### The Sacred Narrative (Kathe):
In the city of Jayavardhana lived a brāhmaṇa of good conduct named Subhadra and his devoted wife Suvratā. Distressed by severe poverty, they departed for the forest.

There, on a riverbank, Suvratā saw women performing the Jyeṣṭhā Lakṣmī Vrata. Following the guidance of sage Vasiṣṭha, she observed the vrata faithfully on Bhādrapada Śukla Aṣṭamī and worshiped the sixteen-knotted sacred cord (*dora*).

### The Divine Fruit & Blessing (Phala):
By the grace of Śrī Mahālakṣmī, their poverty ended. They obtained a fine home filled with wealth and grain, and noble children.

**Phalaśruti:** The booklet says that those who faithfully perform this vrata and hear its story are freed from sins and attain auspicious good fortune.
